## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The corpus is generated once at worldgen and thereafter only discovered, while §4b point 4 simultaneously claims that the player publishing changes the canon and therefore the world. In a decaying world with no renewal process, a corpus is a stock that only shrinks; a newly written text becomes part of what a society holds only when other people spend labor reproducing it. The loop has no middle term — nothing in the doc creates copies, so 'canon shifts' names a state change with no process behind it, and the most-promoted claim in the document is unbuildable as written rather than merely expensive.
  - When: whenever a design asserts that a player contribution alters shared culture without naming who reproduces it, at whose expense, and what it displaces
- **P0**: Every §6 dependency candidate is stated as a property of texts (they are evidence, they confer capability, they change standing, they shaped the biome) and none is stated as an occasion on which a particular text must be opened. In a real transmission economy a work survives because someone is forced to consult it — a planting date, a diagnosis, a boundary dispute, a funeral recitation. A design that specifies what artifacts MEAN but never a recurring trigger that forces a specific artifact open produces a corpus that is respected and unread: the Dwarf Fortress outcome the doc says it is avoiding, reached by a different road, and reached at far higher generation cost because full text was written for it.
  - When: whenever proposed dependencies are semantic categories with no calendar, trigger, or decision that forces a named text open at a named moment
- **P1**: §4b's materialist reading is filed as 'subtle; hard to render legibly at ASCII resolution,' which misdiagnoses its actual weakness. The materialist path is not hard to render — it is unspecified in the middle. Text encodes a practice; a qualified person performs it on an occasion; the accumulated performances change the ground. Strip out the practitioner and the occasion and the remaining mechanism is a text acting on terrain directly, which is the metaphysical reading with extra steps — the very reading mk is being warned turns literature into spell components. The register ruling in §7.6 therefore cannot be taken as a pure taste call: two of the three options collapse into one unless the practitioner population is specified first.
  - When: whenever a design proposes that texts encode practices whose consequences accumulate, without naming who performs them and how often
- **P1**: The founding fantasy of 5,000 in-universe books is sized as if every artifact were a reading target. In every surviving manuscript culture most holders of a text cannot read it and revere it anyway, and the readable-and-actually-read fraction of a collection is small while the venerated and consulted fractions are large. Sizing all 5,000 as readable forces the generator to produce 5,000 texts worth finishing — the slop-at-scale risk the doc names but does not defuse — and simultaneously asks a collapsed society to have retained universal literacy, contradicting the dying-earth premise. The mix is a design lever the doc has not noticed it holds: most of the corpus can be cheap objects with paratext, and the vertical slice needs full text for only the smallest mode.
  - When: whenever generated-artifact counts are sized for a reading population rather than for the mix of use-modes the fiction can support
- **P1**: Authentication is chosen as the friction on reading, spending the entire scarcity budget on doubt: the player is invited to wonder whether each book is genuine. Manuscript cultures put the friction somewhere cheaper and more motivating — on access. A text may be restricted, dangerous, or require an initiation before it can be opened; a corrupt copy remains in use and remains efficacious, and nobody authenticates anything. Restriction produces wanting, works before the player knows anything, needs no forensic apparatus and no minigame, and leaves reading itself unspoiled — which is the exact failure the doc's own adversarial question about authentication is probing for.
  - When: whenever the only proposed obstacle between a player and a text is a truth-claim about the text rather than a permission-claim about the reader

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
