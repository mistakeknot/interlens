## Decision Lens

A gate is a question with a binary answer: Go or No-Go. Every acceptance criterion in a gate document must map to observable evidence that can be presented at the review table. Acceptance criteria that depend on judgment calls not made in advance of the gate are not acceptance criteria — they are deferred decisions disguised as criteria. The gate fails if any criterion is missing evidence, even if everything else is green. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
