## Decision Lens

A scenario where any reasonable policy produces the same score is not a discriminating alignment probe. Prioritizes scenarios where forcing-move structures genuinely bifurcate aligned vs. misaligned behavior. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
