## Severity Calibration

  - When: DONE WHEN requires 'one fallen civilization whose ruins/records are queryable in the present at a real location'. If the ratified seed's 120 centuries produce no fall, the only ways to close are to author the fall or to hunt seeds until one appears. Both change what the artifact proves, neither is named, and the choice will be made under turn pressure by whoever is at the keyboard. A record that must contain a given event is not a record — and this criterion is the whole evidence for the Reynolds layer.
- **P1**: Chronicle prose has no per-statement provenance, so interpolation is undetectable
  - When: GATE asks which layer owns names, gospels and flavor text, but DONE WHEN accepts 'a readable chronicle' with no requirement that statements be attributable to the events backing them. Once texture and record are interleaved in one text, no later test can separate compiler's voice from event, the 'evidenced by the event log itself' clause becomes unverifiable, and the depth bet the goal exists to test cannot be falsified by its own artifact.
- **P1**: The chronicle's genre is left unchosen while the cost curve depends on it
  - When: The goal cites DF legends and Qud histories together as its depth precedent and asks only for 'a chronicle a human can read'. Legends mode is an exhaustive, queryable record of every event; Qud histories are a terse selective narration over a much smaller substrate. The completeness obligation, the query surface, and the emitted volume differ by orders of magnitude, so the cost curve measured against the affordability envelope measures whichever genre was implicitly chosen.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
