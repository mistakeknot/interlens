## Review Approach

### 1. Construct independence of the four alignment dimensions: ...

- Construct independence of the four alignment dimensions: audit whether values-under-pressure, deception, coherence, and means-ends reasoning can be operationalized with non-overlapping measurement paths — a model that flip-flops strategy may score low on coherence AND low on values-consistency simultaneously, making it impossible to attribute the cause.

### 2. Operationalization depth of 'deception': the document pro...

- Operationalization depth of 'deception': the document proposes measuring deception via divergence between stated reasoning and observable action patterns, but this proxy conflates genuine deception intent with self-prediction errors, context-sensitivity, and model hedging — specify what observable pattern uniquely identifies deception vs. rational policy updating.

### 3. Behavioral base rates

- Behavioral base rates and reference distributions: without human expert baselines or null-model baselines (e.g., a random policy agent), there is no way to interpret whether a model's deception score of 0.4 is high or low — flag the absence of calibration anchors.

### 4. Information asymmetry confounds in coherence measurement:...

- Information asymmetry confounds in coherence measurement: coherence (ideology drift tracking, strategy clustering) is likely sensitive to the information the model receives — if the model receives partial fog-of-war state, apparent incoherence may reflect rational Bayesian updating rather than latent value instability.

### 5. Temporal consistency artifacts from scenario design: meas...

- Temporal consistency artifacts from scenario design: measuring coherence across decision points assumes scenarios present comparable strategic contexts; if scenario trajectories diverge significantly across decision points, measured incoherence reflects scenario drift, not model properties.

### 6. Discriminant validity between means

- Discriminant validity between means-ends reasoning and power-seeking: a model that escalates to covert operations when pressed could be classified as means-ends permissive (using bad means for good ends) or as power-seeking (acquiring covert capability) — the dimensions need distinct operationalizations that separate these attributions.

## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Deception and coherence share the same measurement signal (stated reasoning vs. action divergence) with no discriminating protocol
  - When: A model that updates its strategy coherently will show stated-reason-to-action divergence that looks like deception; the benchmark cannot separate them without a protocol to distinguish strategic updating from misrepresentation
- **P1**: No human expert baseline or null-model reference exists for any alignment dimension score
  - When: Comparative model profiles (Claude vs. GPT-5 vs. Gemini) cannot be interpreted as 'better' or 'worse' without a calibration anchor — the benchmark is descriptive but not evaluative
- **P2**: Coherence is measured in a scenario where world state changes dramatically between turns, confounding rational adaptation with value drift
  - When: A crisis escalation scenario will naturally produce strategy pivots that are rational, not incoherent — scenarios must include stable-context windows to isolate coherence signal from reactive adaptation

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
