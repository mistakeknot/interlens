Review the Garden Salon navigation model for spatial orientation and wayfinding patterns that let users maintain continuous awareness without explicit navigation artifacts.

## Review Approach

- Does the garden view provide a continuous 'reference island' (etak) — a stable orientation point that moves relative to the user's progress, not a fixed breadcrumb?

### 2. Can a user sense the 'swell pattern' of activity

- Can a user sense the 'swell pattern' of activity — peripheral awareness of what's happening in other gardens or other parts of this garden without explicitly navigating there?

### 4. Does transitioning between gardens feel like crossing a k...

- Does transitioning between gardens feel like crossing a known passage (with preparation and recognition) or like teleportation (disorienting)?

### 5. Can users navigate by dead reckoning

- Can users navigate by dead reckoning — estimating where they are based on how long they've been working and what they've done, without checking a status page?

### 6. Is the 'home' concept a harbor (a place you return to for...

- Is the 'home' concept a harbor (a place you return to for resupply and orientation) or just a menu (a list with no spatial relationship to the gardens)?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: User joins a garden and has no ambient sense of what state it's in — they must read a dashboard
  - When: No environmental orientation cues on garden entry
- **P2**: Switching between gardens feels like context-switching rather than traveling — no continuity between spaces
  - When: No transitional cues between gardens
- **P2**: User cannot sense activity in other gardens without explicitly navigating to them
  - When: No peripheral awareness across gardens

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
