Real editorial work involves mixed edits — some unchanged, some restructured, some added. All-delete transforms may signal 'this tool replaces bad AI output' rather than 'this tool elevates your own rough drafts', which creates positioning confusion for the freelancer persona who doesn't self-identify as using AI.
- **P2**: Label taxonomy doesn't match freelancer workflow language
  - When: Freelancers typically categorize by deliverable and client type, not content format. 'Blog intro' and 'LinkedIn post' are format labels; working professionals may use project-type language ('client briefing', 'thought leadership draft', 'SEO article lede'). The generic format labels may signal outsider knowledge of the space.

  - When: The nav tagline is hidden on mobile ('hidden sm:inline') and requires familiarity with editorial vocabulary ('intent' as purposeful vs. accidental writing) to parse correctly. A content manager without editorial background may read 'intent' as a legal or SEO term rather than a craft term.
