## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A required kill from a free flight
  - When: OUTCOME commits that the civilizations' past is SIMULATED through palettice rather than authored as text. DONE WHEN then requires that the generated history include, at least, civilizations rising with derived facets, era-scale events among contact, schism, war, extinction or sublimation, and one fallen civilization whose ruins and records are queryable in the present at a real location. Those two sentences cannot both be load-bearing. A falconer can put a hawk in yarak over good ground and can promise nothing about today; if a kill has been promised to an audience, the kill will be produced, and it will be produced by a forcing rule, by reflying until a flight works, or by a hand. Each of those is available here and each destroys the claim: a genesis rule that guarantees a collapse makes the fall scheduled rather than emergent; keeping the seed that happened to produce one makes the demonstration a selection over attempts rather than a property of the pipeline; and writing the fall makes it the authored text the OUTCOME rules out. The condition does not choose between them, does not say which are acceptable, and does not say what happens on a run where no civilization falls — so the choice will be made under demonstration pressure by whoever is at the keyboard on the day, and whichever they choose the goal will be reported as met.
- **P0**: Nothing here can be satisfied by a surprise
  - When: Read every DONE WHEN bullet as a question about what would count. Each names its own outcome in advance: a dated doc with rulings, a deterministic scaffold of a few hundred systems, an end-to-end run producing a readable chronicle, a history containing three named kinds of event, a cost curve against a named envelope, a green workspace test. Not one of them can be satisfied by something nobody specified. The only observational sentence in the document — report what was generated, measured, and still assumed — is attached to the stop rule as a reporting duty, not as an acceptance criterion. So the goal cannot register its own thesis: a run that generated twelve thousand years containing something nobody had thought to ask for, and none of the three listed event kinds, fails; a run that produced precisely the three listed kinds and nothing else passes and is written up as evidence that the depth bet is sound. This is a goal about emergence whose entire acceptance surface is a specification of the outcome, and the effect is not on the review — it is on what gets built, because the pipeline will be built to the surface that can pass.
- **P0**: A field with nothing in it to catch
  - When: GATE asks how thin the physical substrate can be and answers the question entirely in terms of consumers: star map with spectral class and habitability tags, given that Dixon evolution consumes planetary parameters and Reynolds ruins need coordinates. That bounds thinness by what downstream code will read. It never asks the other question, which is whether the resulting world contains anything for a history to be about. Contact, schism and war are not properties of civilizations in isolation; they arise from proximity, scarcity, a chokepoint, a thing two parties both want and cannot both have. A few hundred systems tagged with spectral class and habitability, with homeworlds, neighborhoods and contact reach derived from them, may be a perfectly sufficient input format for every consumer named and still be a field with no quarry — in which case the era-scale events the criteria require will have to come from somewhere other than the world, which is to say from the genesis rules, which is to say scheduled. The failure is invisible to every fork in the GATE, because every fork is asked from the side of what the code needs to read rather than from the side of what the simulation needs to hunt in.
- **P1**: The lure-bound pipeline
  - When: A bird flown only to the lure becomes superb at the lure. It stoops on cue, it returns, it is a pleasure to watch, and it cannot take live game — and the falconer flying it is not lying, because everything the audience sees is real. The probe specified here will be built against a list: a chronicle a human can read, events citing real places, civilizations rising with derived facets, three named event kinds, one fallen civilization with queryable ruins. Every one of those is a lure — a known, named, swung target. A pipeline that produces exactly them, reliably, from any seed, satisfies the condition completely and demonstrates nothing about whether the engine generates history. The tell would be a run producing something on the list that nobody arranged for, or something off the list at all, and no criterion in the document asks for either. Nothing distinguishes a pipeline that hunts from one that has learned the shape of its own acceptance criteria, and the goal exists to answer exactly that question.

  - When: The condition ends with a hard recall: stop after 20 turns and report. The stop rule here is written as an honesty measure, and without any statement of what a partial return looks like it functions instead as pressure to reach the whole list before turn 20 by whatever route is quickest, which is the route through the lure.

A falconer weighs the bird before every flight, and last week's number is not information. The condition treats the figure as settling something durable about the engine, and says nothing about the reading having to be retaken before it is relied on, or about what would make it invalid.
