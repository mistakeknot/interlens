## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Headline requires inference to understand what 'rough' and 'ready' mean
  - When: A first-time visitor who has never seen AI-slop copy cannot decode the transformation without watching the animation — headline alone fails the 5-second test
- **P2**: No subheadline bridges headline to form
  - When: Visitor understands the headline conceptually but has no confirmation of who this is for (freelancers vs. enterprises) before being asked to commit email
- **P3**: 'Join the waitlist' CTA is cost-framed
  - When: Button copy positions the action as waiting in a queue rather than securing access; rephrasing to 'Get early access' or 'Reserve my spot' typically lifts CTR 8–15% in comparable SaaS waitlist tests

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
