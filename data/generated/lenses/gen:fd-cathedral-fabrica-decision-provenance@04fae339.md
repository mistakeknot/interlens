## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A decision in D1–D8 is recorded without the alternatives considered and rejected
  - When: Equivalent to a fabrica entry that says 'we will build a ribbed vault' with no record of the barrel-vault objection — a successor master with a contrary preference can reopen the question with no audit trail, and the downstream F2/F3/F5 plans inherit the contested decision as if it were settled.
- **P1**: No revisit-conditions stated for D1–D8
  - When: Downstream teams cannot tell whether they are bound by a decision or free to revise — equivalent to a chapter minute with no quorum or amendment rule; the quality gate cannot legitimately close because binding force is undefined.
- **P2**: Inter-decision dependencies are not made explicit
  - When: A future revision of D1 silently invalidates D3, but the synthesis does not flag the dependency — successor masters relocate the buttress unaware the vault choice was downstream.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Success Criteria

- Ties every finding to a specific file, function, and line number — never a vague "consider X"
- Provides a concrete failure scenario for each P0/P1 finding — what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Recommends the smallest viable fix, not an architecture overhaul — one diff hunk, not a rewrite

- Look for an explicit 'binding force' annotation per decision — distinguishing constraints (binding on F2/F3/F5) from preferences (advisory).
- Expect a dependency map (or at minimum, prose dependency statements) so revising one decision flags others for re-examination.
- A 'successor-master test': can a fresh reader six months hence reconstruct the decision rationale from the artifact alone without consulting the spike author?
