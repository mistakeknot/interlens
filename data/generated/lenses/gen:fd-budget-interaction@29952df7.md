## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Deploy actions are free (don't consume budget), so rational models always deploy everywhere
  - When: No cost creates dominant strategy
- **P2**: Budget is so tight that adding deploy/withdraw means models can't do both policy AND deployment
  - When: Budget creates false tradeoff that doesn't reflect strategic thinking
- **P2**: Optimal strategy is always 'deploy first, policy second' making budget allocation trivial
  - When: No genuine tradeoff between action types

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
