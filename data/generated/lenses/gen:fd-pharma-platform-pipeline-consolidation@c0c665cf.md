## Decision Lens

Platforms are justified by the products they enable. A platform that generalises ahead of its first validated consumer is building on hypotheses that may not survive contact with the product. The correct sequencing question is not 'platform or product first' but 'what is the minimum platform that enables the first product to validate the platform's core assumptions'. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
