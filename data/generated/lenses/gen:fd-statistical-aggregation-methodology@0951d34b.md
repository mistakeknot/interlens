## Task Context

analyze_cookoff.py and build_anchor_suite.py are the statistical heart of the calibration pipeline -- they transform raw model selections into the anchor suite that drives Auraken's lens selection evaluation. Statistical methodology errors here do not produce loud failures; they produce silently wrong anchors that pass all software tests while encoding misleading calibration signals. ## Review Approach

### 1. Agreement metric appropriateness: Model agreement in a 291-label multi-label

- Agreement metric appropriateness: Model agreement in a 291-label multi-label setting requires a metric that accounts for chance agreement -- raw agreement proportion is inflated because most labels are never selected. Is Fleiss's kappa (or Krippendorff's alpha for multi-label) used, or does the pipeline use raw co-selection rates that over-state agreement?

### 2. Frequency aggregation in multi-label settings: When each model selects 0-3

- Frequency aggregation in multi-label settings: When each model selects 0-3 lenses, the frequency of lens i is a function of both genuine relevance and menu effects (the other lenses presented alongside i). Does the frequency computation account for the menu effect, or does it treat each lens selection as independent when they are structurally correlated?

### 3. Bias-corrected scoring stability: The bias correction weights low-bias models

- Bias-corrected scoring stability: The bias correction weights low-bias models higher. What is the functional form of this weighting, and is the corrected score stable to small changes in the bias coefficient? If two models with similar bias scores receive very different weights due to a discontinuity in the weighting function, the correction itself becomes a source of variance.

### 4. Anchor threshold selection: The threshold separating anchor scenarios (high

- Anchor threshold selection: The threshold separating anchor scenarios (high agreement) from regular scenarios is presumably a hyperparameter. How was it chosen? What is the sensitivity of the anchor set composition to small threshold changes? A threshold chosen by visual inspection of a distribution is less defensible than one chosen by a principled criterion (e.g., top quartile, elbow method).

### 5. Topic coverage as a statistical requirement: The pipeline verifies topic

- Topic coverage as a statistical requirement: The pipeline verifies topic coverage -- but what is the acceptance criterion for adequate coverage? If topic group 14 has 2 anchors out of 200 scenarios, is that a coverage failure or a valid signal that topic 14 has genuinely low consensus? The pipeline must distinguish these cases.

### 6. Confidence intervals for derived metrics: The analysis computes lens frequency

- Confidence intervals for derived metrics: The analysis computes lens frequency, agreement, and bias-correction metrics. Are any confidence intervals reported? With 12 models and 1,360 dilemmas, the effective sample size for per-lens statistics varies enormously -- a lens selected in 5 dilemmas has a very different reliability profile than one selected in 200.

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Raw co-selection rate is used as the agreement metric without chance-correction -- in a 291-label space where models select 0-3 lenses, two models selecting any lens at all is rare enough that raw co-selection rates systematically over-state agreement, causing spurious anchors where models happened to pick the same popular lenses without genuine conceptual alignment.
  - When: No Fleiss kappa or equivalent; agreement = (models selecting lens i / total models) with no correction for marginal selection probability.
- **P1**: The bias-correction weighting function has a sharp discontinuity -- models just above and just below the bias threshold receive dramatically different weights, causing the corrected anchor set to be sensitive to which run (with model-level stochasticity) placed a borderline model above or below the threshold.
  - When: Step function weighting rather than smooth weighting; no sensitivity analysis on threshold placement.
- **P2**: The anchor threshold is a hardcoded magic number (e.g., agreement > 0.7) chosen without documentation, causing the anchor suite to have different composition if the threshold were 0.65 or 0.75 -- downstream calibration quality is sensitive to an arbitrary number with no principled basis.
  - When: No threshold sensitivity analysis, no elbow detection, no documented rationale for threshold value.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
