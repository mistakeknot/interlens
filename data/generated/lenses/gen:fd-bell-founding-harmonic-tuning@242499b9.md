## Task Context

Garden Salon is a multiplayer human-agent collaboration workspace. The review target is the entire project through the lens of campanology: how multiple voices (agents, humans) achieve harmonic coherence when working concurrently on shared artifacts, given that some actions are irreversible.

## Review Approach

### 1. Harmonic coherence of concurrent voices
- In a bell peal, each bell has its own partials but must harmonize with the ensemble. In Garden Salon, each agent proposes independently. What ensures proposals don't create dissonance (conflicting edits that are each locally good but globally incoherent)?
- Bell founders tune partials by shaving metal from the inner surface after casting. The annotation model (approve/reject post-hoc) is post-hoc tuning. But bell tuning is limited — you can only remove metal, never add. What are the limits of post-hoc annotation?
- A peal follows a method (a mathematical pattern). What is the "method" that sequences agent and human contributions? Is there one, or is it purely emergent?

### 2. Irreversibility and the merge moment
- Casting a bell is irreversible — the bronze is committed. Direction merge is described as final (merged directions cannot be reopened). Is the project's merge ceremony as deliberate as a bell founder's decision to pour?
- In campanology, a cracked bell is a total loss. What is the equivalent in Garden Salon — a merge that introduces incoherent state? How is this detected before it's irreversible?
- The "cooling period" for governance transitions mirrors the annealing period after casting. Is the cooling period long enough for the metaphorical metal to set?

### 3. The role of the tuner vs the founder
- The bell founder designs the mold. The tuner adjusts after casting. In Garden Salon, who is the founder (designs the direction/garden) and who is the tuner (adjusts post-hoc)? Is the Owner both?
- Over-tuning a bell (removing too much metal) destroys it. Can over-annotation (too many rejections, too much steering) destroy a direction's coherence?

### 4. Ensemble design
- A carillon is designed as an ensemble — bells are tuned relative to each other, not in isolation. Are directions designed relative to each other, or in isolation? What ensures cross-direction coherence within a garden?

## Severity Calibration

- **P0:** No mechanism prevents globally incoherent state from individually valid proposals
- **P1:** Merge finality has insufficient ceremony for its irreversibility
- **P2:** Post-hoc annotation has undocumented limits
- **P3:** Cross-direction coherence is unaddressed
