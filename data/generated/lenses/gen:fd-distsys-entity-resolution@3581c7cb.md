## Decision Lens

For each variant, ask: what happens to every existing mapping value the day a referenced repo is renamed, forked, merged, or archived — does the field stay silently correct, silently wrong, or does something force a re-check? A field that stores a URL or slug as a bare string is storing a snapshot of an identity claim at write-time, not a live binding to the identity itself, and the review must judge how far that snapshot can drift before anyone notices.
