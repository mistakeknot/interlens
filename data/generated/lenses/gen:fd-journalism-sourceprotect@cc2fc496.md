## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A published fragment contains a phrase distinctive enough to be searchable on the source platform, allowing any reader to find the original profile.
  - When: The transformation step does not include a distinctiveness-suppression check, and the phrase appears verbatim in the published output.
- **P1**: An editorial capsule describes a pattern (e.g., 'a recurring theme of grief and horniness in this cohort') that is, upon inspection, derived from a single fragment rather than a genuine multi-source pattern.
  - When: The editorial workflow does not enforce a minimum-N independent fragments rule before pattern claims are published.
- **P2**: A fragment from a small geographic subculture contains enough scene-specific references that readers within that scene could attribute it to a specific person.
  - When: The tagging and transformation pipeline does not apply additional anonymization to fragments from small or highly recognizable subcultures.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
