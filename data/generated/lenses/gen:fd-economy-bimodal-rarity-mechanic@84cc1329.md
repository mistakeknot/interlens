## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Aesthetic is rolled independently and photo content is player-supplied (a photo the player chooses to submit), so a player who wants a guaranteed rare tail submits a photo engineered to be either extremely on-theme or extremely off-theme for whatever aesthetic they suspect will roll — this is discoverable through a handful of experiments and, once shared, collapses the 'rare' tail into a farmable exploit rather than a surprise
- **P1**: The only tuning lever exposed is the midpoint-distance formula's steepness/threshold, which simultaneously controls both how often a tail is hit AND which specific creature/aesthetic/photo combos qualify as tail — when live-ops wants to make masterpieces rarer without changing which pairings count as masterpieces (a routine post-launch balance pass), there is no way to do that without redefining what 'masterpiece' means, breaking existing player mental models of the mechanic
- **P2**: The design gives no player-facing signal distinguishing 'you got the boring middle' from 'you got a real pull' until the rarity label appears, and because the vast middle is stated to be most of the distribution, most sessions read as flat/unrewarding even though the underlying math is sound — a rarity mechanic that is correct in aggregate can still be a bad moment-to-moment collectible experience if the modal outcome doesn't feel like anything

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
