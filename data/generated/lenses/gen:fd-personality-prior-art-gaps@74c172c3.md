## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Continuous axes produce 'mush' — most elves feel similar because extreme values are rare and moderate values are indistinguishable
  - When: Normal distribution clusters 68% of elves in the 0.35-0.65 range on each axis
- **P2**: No intra-personality conflict resolution — contradictory impulses from different axes produce undefined behavior
  - When: Two axes recommend opposite actions with no tiebreaker mechanism
- **P3**: Missing the 'trait discovery' moment that makes RimWorld compelling — you learn a pawn is a pyromaniac through their actions, not a stats screen
  - When: Personality is only visible through explicit UI display, never through emergent behavior

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
