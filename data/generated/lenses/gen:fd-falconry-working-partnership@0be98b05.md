## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: §3 specifies Uncs as equipped Delve skills with a capacity, a loadout, and swaps between runs — a roster that is present whenever the player opens the game. But uncrancher's own locked decision of 2026-05-22 (the bird-feeder reframe, in the vision doc's decision table and PRD) states that Uncs visit and leave, and that standing is earned as visitors → regulars → residents by visit count plus habitat investment. Those are incompatible availability models and the document never puts them in the same paragraph. Under the feeder model the correct question before every Delve is 'who is here right now,' and the answer is probabilistic and decays with time since the last walk; §3 presumes the answer is always 'everyone you have ever met.' The composition also never states whether an Unc who has stopped visiting is still deployable.
  - When: When §3's slot-inheritance argument is accepted on the strength of the mechanics table alone. If availability is intermittent, the loadout, the capacity number, the bond curve, and §9-B's entire framing are all built on a roster that the locked model does not provide — and if availability is instead made permanent, the bird-feeder reframe has been silently reversed without appearing anywhere in the §10 contradiction audit.
- **P1**: §2.1 identifies the chore-queue failure — slow layer gated on fast layer — and rules it out. It does not consider the failure that killed falconry as a working practice within two generations of the arrival of cheap firearms: nothing was gated, the fast method simply produced the same yield while requiring no weather, no daily upkeep, and no walking, and the slow practice survived only as ceremony performed by enthusiasts. This composition has the same shape. The Delve is seated, indoors, repeatable at will, ~20 minutes, and is the only tempo that mints objects with rarity legible at a glance (§5.3). The walk yields exactly one Unc, requires a body outdoors, and carries a 24-hour re-lure cooldown. §8.3 removes the gate in the protective direction only; there is no mechanism anywhere in the document that makes the walk the thing the player wants to do rather than the thing the player did once.
  - When: When §9-B Option 1 is taken as the answer. A one-time acquisition gate is fully satisfied by a player who walks once and then Delves for a year, which produces exactly the sitting-only game ontology §5 forbids while technically honoring the clause. Report this as a design failure independent of whether the clause is amended.
- **P2**: §4 rests on the claim that 'the mechanic is identical either way (person × object → ability); the possession vector is reversed.' In the trade this distinction is settled by one question: after the flight, does the partner keep anything. A cormorant on the Nagara River is handed fish and cannot swallow the large ones; the giving is real and the possession is the fisherman's. The document never states whether an artifact given to an Unc can be reclaimed, re-given, or optimized across the roster between Delve runs, and §3.1 approvingly imports 'switch only between Delve runs' from a slot specification that also contains a Trading System, a bond reset on trade, and Bond Transfer items — none of which appear in §3.1's table or in §10's contradiction audit.
  - When: When §4's inversion is treated as settled by §8.5. If the gift is revocable and the Unc is tradeable, the possession vector was never reversed; it was renamed, and ontology §2.12 plus §5's commodification prohibition apply to the result exactly as they did before. If the gift is genuinely irrevocable, that is a real and expensive ruling and it is currently unwritten.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
