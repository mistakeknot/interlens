## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: App is force-killed by iOS mid-write to the session manifest during a lifecycle event; on relaunch the manifest is truncated/corrupt and the session's prior artifacts become unreferenced with no recovery path

- **P1**: AVAssetWriter silently stalls (e.g. disk contention) for several seconds without an inter-buffer PTS gap being registered because the stall happens between the sample buffer callback and the writer append, outside the instrumented gap window
