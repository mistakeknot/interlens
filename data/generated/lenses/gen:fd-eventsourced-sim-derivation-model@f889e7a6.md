## Decision Lens

Derivations over an append-only log are only correct if the invalidation strategy is complete. Incomplete invalidation produces stale derived state that looks valid. In a century-scale simulation, an unbounded event log with derivations computed on read is a latency time-bomb — not on day one, but after the first 500-year campaign. The question is not whether the derivation model is elegant (it is) but whether its performance assumptions survive the load described in the North Star.
