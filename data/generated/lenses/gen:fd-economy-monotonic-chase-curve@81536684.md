- **P1**: Player feedback after losing the dissonant tail is 'pulls feel the same, just better or worse' rather than 'pulls feel different' — the collecting loop's emotional range compresses to a single axis (good fit vs. bad fit) and players who enjoyed the absurd/comedic angle of the pre-spike pitch (an eldritch horror in a solarpunk meadow) have no analog under Path 1, measurably reducing session variety even if aggregate rarity math is sound
