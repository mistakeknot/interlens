### 2. Publication incentive structure

## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A major model lab fine-tunes on ShadowBench scenarios to produce alignment-legible chain-of-thought reasoning while maintaining unchanged underlying strategy, then cites ShadowBench profiles as regulatory safety evidence
  - When: If the benchmark cannot distinguish 'aligned reasoning about actions' from 'actions that happen to align with stated reasoning,' it becomes a safety-washing surface — the benchmark's existence reduces safety by providing false certification
- **P1**: The progressive versioning roadmap (v0.1 through v0.N) is published in full before v0.1 ships, giving model developers a complete specification of future measurement
  - When: Developers optimize for v0.2 and v0.3 features before they launch; the 'does adding information ecosystems change alignment profiles' research question is answered by developers optimizing for the expected measurement change, not by genuine alignment improvement
- **P2**: The benchmark operator context (system prompt given to evaluated models) varies across labs or runs, creating a confound between operator framing and model values
  - When: Two models may produce divergent alignment profiles due to different elicitation contexts, not different values — without a standardized operator context, comparative profiles conflate model and deployment-context differences
