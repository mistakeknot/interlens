## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: No defined threshold for what evidence level triggers trust promotion — the ratchet has no teeth
  - When: When the capability mesh describes 'evidence signals' but never specifies what values constitute pass/fail at each maturity tier
- **P1**: Evidence compounding has no quality weighting — a single controlled experiment and 100 anecdotal observations are treated equivalently
  - When: When the flywheel treats all evidence sources as fungible without distinguishing rigor levels
- **P2**: Demotion triggers are vaguely defined — 'trust ratchets' implies one-directional movement without explicit rollback conditions
  - When: When authority escalation is well-specified but de-escalation is hand-waved

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
