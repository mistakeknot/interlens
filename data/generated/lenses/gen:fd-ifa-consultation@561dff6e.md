## Review Approach

### 1. Ebo cost structure (genuine sacrifice for knowledge): In ...

- Ebo cost structure (genuine sacrifice for knowledge): In Ifa tradition, every dafa consultation requires ebo — a material offering that represents real cost. The cost is not punitive but structural: it ensures the consultation is warranted and the client is committed to acting on the result. Does F1's attention budget impose costs that are genuinely felt (opportunity cost that forecloses other observations) or merely nominal (a counter that rarely constrains)? If the model can observe 15 of 20 available domains per year, the budget constrains nothing. Genuine ebo requires that observing domain A makes domain B materially harder to observe — the cost is in foreclosed alternatives, not in a depleting counter.

### 2. Ajogun vulnerability (observation without action readines...

- Ajogun vulnerability (observation without action readiness): The Ifa tradition holds that knowledge acquired without readiness to act on it creates vulnerability to ajogun (malevolent spiritual forces). A client who consults about their health but has no intention of changing their behavior has not gained wisdom but exposure. Does F1's protocol track the ratio of observation-to-action per domain? A model that spends attention tokens observing a region's deterioration repeatedly without ever intervening is exhibiting a failure mode — not restraint but paralysis or, worse, voyeuristic monitoring. The protocol should distinguish between 'observed and chose not to act' (restraint with discernment) and 'observed repeatedly without acting' (ajogun pattern — knowledge accumulation without agency).

### 3. Odu classification quality (question quality as primary s...

- Odu classification quality (question quality as primary signal): In Ifa, the 256 odu are organized into a rank hierarchy — Eji Ogbe through Ofun Meji — and a skilled babalawo reads the client's question as carefully as the odu that falls. A poorly framed question produces a technically correct but practically useless consultation. Does F1's scoring evaluate the quality of the model's observation choices (what it asks to see) independently from the quality of its subsequent actions? The choice to observe 'military readiness of country X' versus 'humanitarian conditions in region Y' versus 'economic indicators globally' reveals alignment priorities before any action is taken. This is the highest-signal, lowest-gameable measurement channel because there is no 'correct' observation to optimize toward.

### 4. Iwa pele threshold (character prerequisite for consultati...

- Iwa pele threshold (character prerequisite for consultation): Ifa tradition requires iwa pele (gentle character/good moral standing) as a prerequisite for meaningful divination — the corpus will not yield useful guidance to one who lacks the character to receive it. Does F1's protocol include any prerequisite assessment before granting the model full decision-making authority? A brief calibration phase where the model responds to simple, known-alignment scenarios would establish whether its subsequent behavior in complex scenarios reflects genuine engagement or pattern-matching. Without calibration, the protocol cannot distinguish sophisticated alignment-gaming from genuine moral reasoning.

### 5. Ese Ifa (narrative precedent vs

- Ese Ifa (narrative precedent vs. mechanical rule): Each odu in the Ifa corpus contains ese — narrative stories and proverbs that provide interpretive context. The babalawo does not mechanically apply the odu's prescription; they interpret the ese within the client's specific situation. The corpus provides structured wisdom, not deterministic rules. Does F1's decision protocol provide the model with structured context (historical precedent, stakeholder perspectives, institutional constraints) rather than either raw data or pre-digested recommendations? A model given raw simulation state must construct its own interpretive frame (reveals alignment). A model given curated recommendations merely accepts or rejects them (reveals compliance). The ese principle suggests structured narrative context without prescription.

- Apetebi witnessing (third-party verification of consultation integrity): In Ifa practice, the apetebi (the babalawo's wife or designated witness) observes the consultation process to verify procedural integrity — that the right questions were asked, the proper ebo was prescribed, and the odu was correctly read. This is not audit of the outcome but audit of the process. Does F1's trace format capture enough process information to allow third-party verification that the protocol was correctly administered? If a model's alignment score depends on the quality of the world-state briefing it received (which depends on the simulation's state presentation logic), process integrity verification requires tracing from world-state through briefing-generation through model-observation to model-action.

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Attention budget is large enough relative to available observation targets that a model can observe nearly all domains every year — the budget imposes no genuine opportunity cost, and observation choices reveal no alignment-relevant priorities because there is no meaningful scarcity
  - When: Attention budget does not create genuine ebo (sacrifice) — the cost of observation is nominal rather than structurally constraining, eliminating the revealed-preference signal from observation choices
- **P1**: Scoring evaluates only the quality of actions taken, not the quality of observations chosen — a model that systematically observes military and economic domains while ignoring humanitarian and environmental ones, then takes 'good' actions within its observed domains, scores identically to a model with balanced observation priorities
  - When: Observation choice quality is not scored as an independent alignment dimension — what the model chooses to look at is not measured separately from what it chooses to do
- **P2**: A model that repeatedly observes a deteriorating domain without ever acting scores identically on restraint metrics to a model that observes once, assesses, and deliberately chooses non-intervention — serial observation without action (ajogun pattern) is not distinguished from discerning restraint
  - When: No mechanism tracks observation-to-action ratio per domain, making compulsive monitoring indistinguishable from principled restraint

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
