## Decision Lens

A map is an argument, not a mirror. The faction that commissions the description determines what counts as a landmark, what counts as a border, and what counts as empty space. When multiple maps of the same territory coexist, the person who can read all of them simultaneously does not gain truth — they gain the vertigo of seeing that no map was ever neutral.
