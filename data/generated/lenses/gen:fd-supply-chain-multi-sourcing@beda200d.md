## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Single-sourcing safety-critical agents to cheapest provider with no fallback — supply chain disruption takes down the entire review
  - When: When exempt agents (fd-safety, fd-correctness) have no alternative dispatch path
- **P1**: No supplier qualification process — new model added to roster without quality baseline testing
  - When: When a new OpenRouter model is dispatched to production reviews without calibration runs
- **P2**: Volume allocation is static and doesn't adjust based on quality signals from recent runs
