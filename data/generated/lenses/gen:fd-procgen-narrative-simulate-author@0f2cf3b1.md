## Decision Lens

A chronicle that is fully simulated is not automatically readable, and 'depth' that players can't perceive isn't depth they'll credit — DF's own legends mode is the standard cautionary tale of mechanically correct, humanly unreadable history. A goal that stakes its OUTCOME on DF/Qud depth needs a legibility and texture bar, not just an event-citation bar, or it will satisfy its own DONE WHEN while reproducing DF's known failure mode.
