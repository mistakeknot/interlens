## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: A theme's cycle_time oscillates around the 20% threshold — crossing it, triggering INFORM, offset adjusts, metric improves, signal clears, offset reverts, metric degrades, signal fires again — producing a rapid fire/clear/fire cycle that generates dozens of interspect events per day without achieving stable resolution
  - When: When the INFORM trigger threshold equals the clear threshold (no hysteresis), allowing metrics that hover near the boundary to oscillate between signaled and cleared states
- **P2**: All themes individually show healthy pleasure signals, but the factory as a whole is slower than last week — each theme is within its own baseline but the baselines have all subtly drifted downward, equivalent to a carillon where every bell meets its individual specification but the aggregate temperament has shifted flat
  - When: When pleasure signals are evaluated only per-theme with no factory-level aggregate check, allowing systemic drift that affects all themes equally to be invisible because no individual theme crosses its threshold
- **P3**: Weight-drift detection fires for theme A, advisory offset reduces A's dispatch weight, causing more beads to flow to theme B — theme B's cycle_time baseline shifts due to increased load, causing B's pleasure signal to degrade, producing a cascade where fixing A's drift causes B's drift
  - When: When advisory offset adjustment for one theme changes bead flow rates to other themes, but other themes' baselines are not recalculated to account for the changed workload distribution

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
