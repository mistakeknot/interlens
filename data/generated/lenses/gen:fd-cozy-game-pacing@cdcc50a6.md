## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Option C fills every walk with content, removing negative space
  - When: If every walk reliably produces 3–4 encounters, players lose the experience of walking without agenda — the game trains attention toward encounter management rather than environmental presence, conflicting with the 'anti-Pokémon-GO maximalism' aesthetic statement.
- **P2**: Option D zero-encounter session with no ambient feedback
  - When: If the app shows nothing after a 3km lure-walk with no Unc (no distance logged, no lure 'charge' displayed, no world-state feedback), the session feels like a broken app experience rather than a designed 'quiet day' — the empty session must be legible as intentional.
- **P3**: Guaranteed reward (Option A) collapses anticipation arc over time
  - When: After 20 walks, a player who always gets exactly one Unc loses the low-level tension that makes arrival feel like arrival — the walk becomes a commute with a known destination. This is survivable at v0 but becomes a design debt that is expensive to reverse once players have built habits.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
