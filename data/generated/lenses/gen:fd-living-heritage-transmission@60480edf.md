## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The harvest accessions all ~4,750 agents without distinguishing work made to be reused from work that was complete when its round ended — -fusion-N lenses written against one heat state, siblings shaped by an anti-overlap contract with lenses that are not being offered alongside them, probes generated to be thrown away. Reuse-before-regenerate then offers these by embedding similarity to targets they were never about. The lens reads as competent, because it was competent once, for something else; it displaces a fresh one, and nothing in the system can say so, because the artifact kept its form and lost only its occasion.
  - When: Flag as P0 when the design treats every stored artifact as equally offerable and provides no field, accession rule, or category separating work made for a single occasion from work made to be reused.
- **P1**: Every completion criterion in the record is a property of the collection, so the project can pass all of them — census reconciled, dedupe reported, harvest verified, sweep report complete — delete the .claude/agents piles across roughly thirty live repos, and still have no way to detect that reuse made reviews worse. The first evidence would be ledger findings months later, attributed to individual lenses rather than to the reuse policy, by which point the sources are gone and the comparison against generating fresh can no longer be run.
  - When: Flag as P1 when a design's completion criteria are all properties of the store and none is a property of the work the store exists to improve, particularly where an irreversible step sits behind those criteria.
- **P2**: Once the registry is consulted before writing and embodies edges are suggested by match against the curated definitions, the generator's output drifts toward what the register recognises: lenses that resemble existing entries are surfaced, reused and reinforced, lenses that fit no curated pattern have to justify themselves against a corpus that now defines what a lens looks like, and melange's steer-wide rounds are answered from inside the taxonomy. The catalogue stops describing the practice and starts prescribing it, and no artifact in the design would show it happening.
