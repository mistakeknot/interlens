## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Single-source story from unreliable stringer runs without verification — equivalent to a P0 finding from a cheap model accepted without cross-validation
  - When: When synthesis doesn't flag single-model P0 findings for verification by a higher-quality model
- **P1**: Cross-source validation treats all sources equally regardless of reliability history
  - When: When convergence scoring doesn't weight by provider quality
- **P2**: Editorial attention spread evenly instead of concentrated on high-uncertainty stories
  - When: When Claude verification is applied uniformly instead of targeted at divergent findings

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

When multiple sources report the same story at different quality levels, how do you weight them and where do you invest editorial (high-cost) verification? If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
