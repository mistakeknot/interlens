## Decision Lens

Every ruling that touches persistence is evaluated as a migration step with a before-state, an after-state, and a population of existing writes that has to survive the transition intact or be explicitly declared lost. A plan that names a schema commitment without naming what happens to data written before that commitment lands is treated as incomplete, not merely risky. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
