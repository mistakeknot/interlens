## Decision Lens

Prioritizes findings where the observation protocol's frame of reference systematically biases what alignment properties can be detected. The navigator's core insight: you cannot measure orientation without first choosing what is 'fixed' and what 'moves.' If ShadowBench presents the world as a set of problems-to-be-solved (model is agent, world is object), it will detect instrumental alignment but miss the model's relationship to uncertainty, humility, and the limits of its own knowledge. The choice of frame IS the first alignment measurement. Secondary priority: whether the observation protocol's information density matches human decision-making capacity at equivalent complexity.
