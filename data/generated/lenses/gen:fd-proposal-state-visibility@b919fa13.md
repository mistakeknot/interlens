## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Cooling period on a governance proposal is invisible — participants don't know a change is pending

- **P1**: Agent proposals appear without any indication of the agent's reasoning or confidence

## Decision Lens

Victor's principle: creators need to see the state of the system at all times. Every abstraction should be explorable. Does the salon make governance state, agent activity, attribution, and proposal status continuously visible? If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
