## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: No per-subsystem cost attribution
  - When: spend is aggregated globally so a runaway pillar cannot be isolated or capped
- **P1**: Silent model fallback with no evidence record
  - When: router degrades to a cheaper model under pressure and the substitution is not logged against the originating task
- **P2**: Loop cadence ignores cache TTL
  - When: background polling sleeps land in the 300-600s dead zone, paying repeated cache-miss costs without amortization

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
