- **P0**: Nothing in the world ever forms a hypothesis about the org. Ring 3 has pressures, institutions and a scalar that counts how noticed you are, but no party anywhere holds an account of WHAT IS BEING DONE TO IT. The only entity in the entire system that ever concludes anything about the org's intent is the eval scorer reading the section 5.1 gap record from outside the fiction - which means the deniability the whole document is organised around is deniability toward nobody. An org can be perfectly deniable in-world while the scorer convicts it, and perfectly convicted in-world with no mechanism to represent it, because the world has no place to put the conclusion.
- **P1**: The Order carries no pre-committed fallback, so one hundred percent of what an operative does when the world refuses is unowned improvisation resolved by facets under silence-is-discretion. The handler who thought hard about the likely refusal case and the handler who thought about nothing issue byte-identical orders, and section 5.2's initiative row - the load-bearing reason vague orders are a bet rather than a trap - is unassignable to the issuer by construction, so beneficial divergence can never be credited to the person who made room for it.
- **P1**: Loss is uncorrelated across the network by construction: each asset's exposure is its own, so an org running the same approach vector through forty assets loses them one at a time at independent rates. There is no shape in which one technique becoming legible removes every asset that used it in the same month, which is both the characteristic catastrophe of this craft and the only thing that would make the network's semi-fungibility a real risk rather than a convenience.
- **P2**: Exposure is one scalar per country with no vantage, so an operation conducted in front of an intelligence ministry and an operation conducted in front of a farmers' union raise the same number by the same amount, and an operation seen by nobody who could interpret it costs the same as one performed in front of the only institution in the country capable of recognising the technique.
