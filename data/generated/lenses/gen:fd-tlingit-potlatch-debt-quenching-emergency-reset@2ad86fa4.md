### 2. Transaction freeze during ceremony and the no-new-obligations principle: during

- Transaction freeze during ceremony and the no-new-obligations principle: during the potlatch destruction ceremony, NO new inter-clan obligations can be created — no gift-giving, no debt acknowledgment, no counter-challenge — because new obligations during the quenching process would corrupt the calculus of what is being reset. The Tlingit ceremony closes the social transaction space entirely; the SQLite transaction should close the state-modification space entirely.

- Full quench vs named-debt restoration and the --constrained flag semantics: the Tlingit potlatch offers two outcomes: (1) complete quenching where ALL inter-clan debts are extinguished, or (2) named-debt restoration where specific debts are explicitly revived from the witness record while the rest are considered dead. This is the potlatch without a witness record: the debts are quenched but no one remembers what they were.
