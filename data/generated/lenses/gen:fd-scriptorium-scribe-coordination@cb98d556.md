## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A rate-limited backend causes work to silently route to a low-quality fallback with no flag on the output, producing editorial results that look complete but are substantially worse
  - When: Fallback routing has no quality metadata attached to the output and no downstream check
- **P1**: AIMD backoff after transient rate limits keeps a high-quality backend underutilized for extended periods while cheaper backends handle quality-sensitive work
