## Decision Lens

Sem-enna-Worq calibration — every intervention must carry a 'wax' (surface utility the user consciously receives) and a 'gold' (deeper cognitive restructuring the user discovers through their own interpretive work). The ratio between them must be tuned to the user's current capacity. Near-miss failures occur when the gold is too shallow (user sees through it, feels patronized) or too deep (user takes only the wax, misses the transformation). If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
