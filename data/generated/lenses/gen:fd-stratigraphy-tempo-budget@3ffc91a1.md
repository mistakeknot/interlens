## Decision Lens

Severity tracks whether the failure is structural or tunable. A claimed accumulation with no supply path, or a thesis contradicted by the document's own mechanics table, is P0/P1 — tuning cannot fix a missing driver. An unstated but obviously settable rate is P2. Weight by how many downstream decisions rest on the unexamined quantity: a mis-set drop rate is a knob, but a slow layer with no clock of its own invalidates the three-tempo frame. Prefer naming the missing quantity. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
