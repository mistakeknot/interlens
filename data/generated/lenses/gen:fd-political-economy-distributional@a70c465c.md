## Decision Lens

A taxonomy that models aggregate flows (GDP, trade volume, energy production) without modeling WHO benefits and WHO loses will miss the mechanism that converts economic change into political crisis. Distributional conflict must be a first-class concept, not an emergent byproduct. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
