- **P0**: P4 is adopted as a gate or a heavily-weighted axis and applied to the whole master pool, so that every one of the 60-80 developed domains is selected for its capacity to be read oppositely by rival traditions. In any tradition of disagreement this destroys the thing it was meant to produce: disagreement is perceptible only against settled ground, and a corpus in which every object is contested reads not as a world of rival readings but as a world with no facts, where the traditions are arbitrary label-generators and the reader stops attending to any of them. The classical constraint is structural, not stylistic — matters definitive in indication do not admit disagreement, and the fraction of such matters is what makes disagreement on the rest legible and worth following.
