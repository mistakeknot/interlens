## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The interface feels like three disconnected apps bolted together (editor, governance panel, attribution dashboard)
  - When: No felt wholeness
- **P1**: Agent presence feels robotic — no warmth, personality, or sense of genuine collaboration
  - When: Mechanical agent interaction
- **P2**: Visual hierarchy is flat — everything is the same size and weight
  - When: Missing levels of scale

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these

- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
