Single-screen waitlist page for a SaaS editorial tool. Target audience: freelancers and content managers. Key conversion event: email submission. The hero animation is the primary product demonstration. No social proof, no pricing, no testimonials on the page.

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Brand name does not communicate category
  - When: If 'Texturaize' reads as a design tool, image editor, or material product rather than a writing tool, the nav tagline 'Writing with intent.' is doing critical disambiguation work that most visitors will never read on mobile.
- **P2**: Animation requires patience the visitor hasn't yet granted
  - When: The 200ms showing-before phase followed by 1000ms pause means the visitor sees undifferentiated text for 1.2 seconds before the redline begins. A visitor who hasn't committed to browsing may read the before-text as the product output and leave.
- **P3**: Success state lacks retention hook
  - When: The 'Share this' affordance appears only after conversion and relies on navigator.share, which is unsupported on desktop. A luxury boutique hands the customer a branded bag at checkout — the success state is a missed brand moment.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
