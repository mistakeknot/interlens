## Decision Lens

A rule set that cannot be consistently adjudicated by independent session facilitators without consulting the designer is not a published product — it is a draft, and should be labeled and distributed accordingly. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
