## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Any H1 'historical-affective relationality' effect is confounded with whose affect got archived how — a sociological confound indistinguishable from the intended discourse-mode manipulation, sinking the causal claim regardless of statistical controls.
  - When: The oral-stratum pool systematically skews toward transcribed testimony from marginalized or oral-tradition-dominant communities while the literate-stratum pool skews toward institutionally archived elite prose.
- **P1**: The same oral-history subject appears under different collection identifiers in both the training corpus and the MênisBench test set, producing undetected training-test leakage.
  - When: 'Source group' is defined at the archival-collection level rather than the individual-speaker/event level.
- **P2**: Translated oral sources quietly dominate one arm and skew register independent of orality.
  - When: The corpus contract does not specify whether translation status is an exact-match requirement or a modeled covariate.
