## Decision Lens

Prioritize findings where a gate result is used as a substitute for sustained-rate evidence. The Maintenance Rule distinguishes systems in a-category (continuous performance monitoring required because prior gate results were insufficient) from b-category (periodic inspection sufficient). If Break receipts are scored at a boundary rather than tracked as a rolling rate, the system is claiming b-category treatment for what should be a-category behavior.
