## Task Context

Garden Salon is a multiplayer human-agent collaboration workspace with per-garden governance models, append-only attribution tracking, and an evolutionary path from primitives to templates to constitutions. Agents are first-class participants whose actions land optimistically by default.

## Review Approach

### 1. Direction lifecycle vs protocol amendment workflow

- Does the direction lifecycle map cleanly to a protocol amendment workflow — where active directions under one governance model must be reconciled when the governance model itself changes mid-flight?

### 2. Attribution granularity for initiator vs executor

- Is attribution granular enough to distinguish between 'who initiated the work' and 'who executed the work' — clinical trials require separating the PI who orders a test from the CRA who runs it, just as Garden Salon must separate the human who proposes a direction from the agent that executes it?

### 3. Protocol deviation recording

- Is there a 'protocol deviation' concept — what happens when an agent or participant acts outside the bounds of the current governance model, and is that deviation recorded rather than silently accepted or rejected?

### 4. Audit trail correction model

- Does the append-only attribution model handle corrections the way a clinical audit trail does — not by deleting records but by appending correction entries that reference the original?

### 5. Protocol versioning on governance transitions

- When a garden transitions governance models (Solo to Cooperative), is there a 'protocol version' that freezes the rules under which existing directions were created, preventing retroactive reinterpretation?

### 6. Optimistic defaults vs pre-approval governance

- Is the 'optimistic by default' agent behavior compatible with governance models that require pre-approval — or does the design assume all governance models tolerate post-hoc review?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Governance model change retroactively reinterprets existing attribution
  - When: If a garden switches from Solo to Cooperative and the new voting-weight rules apply to attribution records created under Solo terms, participants who contributed under one social contract are judged by another — this is the clinical-trials equivalent of changing the primary endpoint after unblinding

- **P1**: No protocol deviation recording for out-of-bounds agent actions
  - When: If an agent acts outside its authorized scope in a governed garden and the action is simply accepted or rejected with no record of the deviation, the audit trail has a gap — in clinical trials this is a regulatory finding that can invalidate an entire site's data

- **P2**: Optimistic agent defaults incompatible with pre-approval governance
  - When: The design defaults to 'agent changes land immediately, humans annotate post-hoc' but some governance models (Team, Cooperative) may require approval before merge — if the optimistic default is hardcoded rather than governance-configurable, restrictive governance models cannot enforce their own rules

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
