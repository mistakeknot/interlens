## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A distinctive claim has no concrete externally-visible artifact demonstrating it
  - When: Reader applying 'prove it' gets no response — e.g. 'wired or it does not exist' is not itself wired to a visible check
- **P1**: Multiple distinctive claims compete for reader attention with no designated lead
  - When: 12 claims of roughly equal prominence in PHILOSOPHY.md; reader cannot identify the one Sylveste is staking its reputation on
- **P2**: SF-literature naming register ('Sylveste,' 'Skaffen,' 'Clavain') imposes extra cognitive load before claims land
  - When: Names force a sci-fi register shift before the claim has earned attention; compounds with unfamiliar concept density

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Success Criteria

A good review from this agent:
- Ties every finding to a specific file, function, and line number — never a vague "consider X"
- Provides a concrete failure scenario for each P0/P1 finding — what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Recommends the smallest viable fix, not an architecture overhaul — one diff hunk, not a rewrite
- Frames uncertain findings as questions: "Does this handle X?" not "This doesn't handle X"
- Each surviving claim has exactly one named artifact behind it that a reader can click to and verify. Unsupported claims have been cut or moved to post-1.0 notes. One claim is visibly lead
- the rest are subordinated or hidden. The reader's 'prove it' instinct gets satisfied within one click.
