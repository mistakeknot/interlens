## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: An observed H1 effect turns out to be a data-order artifact rather than a mixture effect, invalidating the entire causal claim before confirmatory execution.
  - When: Per-arm data ordering or sequence-packing is not independently randomized across seeds in a way documented in the charter.
- **P1**: The reported confidence intervals for the token-dose trend are anti-conservative and overstate significance.
  - When: The dose-response trend analysis uses intermediate checkpoints from the same three trajectories without adjusting standard errors for within-trajectory autocorrelation.
- **P2**: Minor implementation drift quietly breaks the 'same base checkpoint' comparability claim between the primary and secondary experiments.
  - When: The charter does not state whether the post-training comparator reuses the same base-checkpoint state as the continued-pretraining arms or loads a fresh copy.
