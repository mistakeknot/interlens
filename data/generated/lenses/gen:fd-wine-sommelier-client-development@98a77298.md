## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Calibration findings are used exclusively to improve the agent's lens selection accuracy without any mechanism for developing the user's own ability to recognize which frameworks apply. The sommelier optimizes their own wine knowledge but never teaches the client to taste.
  - When: Near-miss pairs, distinguishing questions, and contraindications operate entirely within the OODARC Decide phase as internal selection logic, with no pathway for these insights to become user-facing learning moments when the user is ready.
- **P1**: No 'readiness' dimension on contraindications — the system treats contraindications as absolute rather than user-relative. A lens that would be harmful for a new user (who might take the framing too literally) could be perfectly appropriate for an experienced user who can hold the framework lightly.
  - When: The contraindication check operates on situation features alone without incorporating the user's demonstrated capacity to hold frameworks critically, their history of framework internalization, or their current growth edge.
- **P2**: Lens stacks are designed as multi-angle analysis (applying several lenses to provide a more complete picture) rather than as developmental sequences (each lens builds capacity that makes the next lens accessible).
  - When: The lens stack ordering is based on analytical complementarity (which lenses cover different aspects of the problem) rather than pedagogical sequencing (which lens the user needs to internalize first before the next becomes meaningful).

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Success Criteria

A good review from this agent:
- Ties every finding to a specific file, function, and line number — never a vague "consider X"
- Provides a concrete failure scenario for each P0/P1 finding — what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Recommends the smallest viable fix, not an architecture overhaul — one diff hunk, not a rewrite
- Frames uncertain findings as questions: "Does this handle X?" not "This doesn't handle X"
- Near-miss lens pairs are explicitly treated as teaching opportunities — moments when the user can learn to distinguish between similar-looking situations, not just moments when the agent must choose correctly
- Distinguishing questions from the cookoff are designed to be asked OF the user (developing their discernment) not just answered BY the agent (improving its selection)
- The conversation model has a 'growth trajectory' concept that modulates how calibration findings are applied — more scaffolding early, more challenge later, celebration of independence throughout
- Contraindications have a user-relative dimension: the same lens can be contraindicated for a beginner but appropriate for an advanced user who can hold it critically
