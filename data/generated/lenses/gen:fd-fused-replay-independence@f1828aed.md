## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The validator receives the executor's exact worktree and the executor's frozen plate, so its re-run is a perfectly idempotent retry and therefore certifies nothing; the resulting PASS is written to no durable register, so the gauge that produced it can never be assayed and a systematically wrong criterion survives all three pilots undetected.
  - When: One mechanism is asked to deliver both reproducibility and independence, and no durable register exists to reveal which of the two was actually obtained.
- **P1**: An executor subagent never returns. The orchestrator cannot distinguish crash from slow, has no tolerance band for 'no verdict', resolves the silence itself — as the party that authored the criteria — and the two-strikes counter lives only in its context, so nothing records that the item was ever attempted.
  - When: Silence has neither a failure-detector policy nor a remedy band, and the resolution is made by the criterion's author with no durable trace of the criterion it turned on.
- **P2**: A plan waits in the queue while a sibling session lands a change. Its frozen criteria still pass, because they were pinned to nothing and only assert what the plan itself edits; the register, if any, captures the PASS but not that the plate was a week old.
  - When: Frozen criteria carry neither a version pin nor an expiry, and the verdict record omits the plate's age, so staleness is unrecoverable after the fact.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
