## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Animation resolves too completely — the visitor feels informed rather than wanting
  - When: If all three diff examples (blog intro, LinkedIn post, marketing copy) cycle through before the visitor reaches the form, they may feel they've seen the full product range and have no remaining information-gap that joining the waitlist would close.
- **P2**: Peak emotional activation and CTA are not co-located
  - When: The waitlist form is positioned below the animation in DOM order. A visitor experiencing maximum desire at the end of a particularly compelling diff cycle must scroll or shift visual focus to convert — momentum breaks.
- **P3**: HOLD_MS (2500ms) may be insufficient for slower readers to process the after-state
  - When: The marketing copy example produces a 45-word after-sentence. At average adult reading speed, 2500ms hold allows approximately 10-12 words. The visitor may be mid-read when the cycle resets, producing frustration rather than desire.
