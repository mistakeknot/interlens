## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Two mesh cells have a hidden dependency that invalidates the 'independent maturation' claim
  - When: Routing cannot mature without Measurement data, but Measurement is at ~80% and blocked — meaning Routing maturity is silently capped
- **P1**: An evidence signal listed for a cell is not currently collected and has no plan to collect it

- **P2**: The mesh has no summarization mechanism — stakeholders cannot quickly answer 'how mature is the platform?'
  - When: 10 independent cells with no aggregation means the mesh is accurate but not communicable

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
