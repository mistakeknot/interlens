## Task Context

A self-improving editorial AI has 23K before/after editing pairs with 7K editor comments. Phase 2 requires annotation schema design, weak supervision from comments, a defeasible rule hierarchy, and dual-encoder embeddings for editorial similarity.

## Decision Lens

Prioritizes schema designs that are (a) losslessly representable in standard formats for tooling reuse, (b) capable of handling overlapping and hierarchical spans without structural hacks, and (c) compatible with active open-source annotation tooling as of 2025.
