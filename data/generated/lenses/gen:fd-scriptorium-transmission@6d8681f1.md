  - When: Diff shows behavioral divergence between duplicates and neither file references the other as exemplar or deprecated.

## Decision Lens

Every skill, command, and doc is a manuscript copy in a transmission chain. Ask: what is the exemplar (canonical source), how many derivative copies exist, and has copying introduced silent divergence? A corpus that compounds knowledge must keep its cross-references alive the way a codex keeps its concordance alive — otherwise knowledge accretes as isolated folios no reader can navigate.
