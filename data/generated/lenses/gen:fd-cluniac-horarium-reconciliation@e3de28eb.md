## Severity Calibration

- **critical**: A grave drift (agent_mismatch on a governance-bearing session) is emitted at info severity
  - When: reconciler.py severity assignment treats a breach of role identity as equivalent to a bookkeeping note, collapsing the venial/grave distinction
- **warning**: The customary (intended state) is silently rewritten to match observed runtime
  - When: Control-plane config is regenerated from live tmux without a professing step, so drift is erased instead of recorded
- **info**: A legitimate exception must be expressed by removing the intent rather than recording a dispensation

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
