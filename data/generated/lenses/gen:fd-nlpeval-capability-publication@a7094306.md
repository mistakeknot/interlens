## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Researchers decide post hoc whether a mediator explains away an effect after seeing results — a single degree of freedom that can convert a noise finding into a claimed capability effect and invalidate the publication.
  - When: The mediator-attribution rule for H1/H2 (treating a response-style change as an 'explanatory failure') is never specified with a concrete statistical test or threshold before confirmatory analysis.
- **P1**: A spurious H2 'capability profile' difference appears that is actually just register transfer — exactly the confound the charter says must be ruled out.
  - When: The discourse-capability battery scores free-text generation without a constrained-choice or length-matched-decoding protocol.
- **P2**: The frozen power calculation is miscalibrated in either direction, wasting compute budget or leaving the confirmatory run underpowered.
  - When: The variance pilot's bounded token dose is far below the eventual confirmatory dose and no dose-scaling model for variance is specified.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
