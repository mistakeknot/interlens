- **P1**: The affordability envelope is read off a wet wall
  - When: 'the probe reports its own cost curve (systems x civs x members x centuries) against the 2026-08-07 affordability envelope, so the next engine bottleneck is measured rather than guessed' takes its reading in the one configuration the goal guarantees will not persist: minimum substrate, stubbed species, settle mode. Fresco colours dry lighter than they appear wet; these numbers will move when the strata OUTCOME promises to deepen are deepened, and the condition does not ask which axes hold across that change. The stated purpose — bottleneck measured, not guessed — is met only for a wall that will be repainted.
- **P2**: The scaffolding chooses the seams and the goal leaves the scaffolding unruled
  - When: DONE WHEN offers 'watchable in the terminal (civ_scale --watch precedent) or browsable via the mk-67q chronicle path' as an untaken either/or. In fresco the pontate — the horizontal seams left by each lift of the scaffold — are dictated by how the staging was erected, not by the composition, and they are in the wall forever. Which observation surface is built first will fix what the chronicle is shaped to emit; leaving it as an or means the tooling decides a product question by whichever path is cheaper on the day.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
