## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Option D first-week triple empty-walk churn cliff
  - When: A new player who walks three times in their first week with common lures has a 12.5% chance of three empty sessions — this is a quantifiable onboarding failure rate that will manifest as visible D7 churn for a significant cohort. Without a consolation mechanism (a 'near miss' signal, a lore reason for failure), these players attribute failure to the app rather than to the design.
- **P1**: Option C 'felt-complete' arc too short for v0 Unc variety space
  - When: If v0 ships with N Unc families and Option C produces 20–25 encounters per week for dedicated walkers, the expected time to 'I've seen everything' is N/20 weeks — if that number is under 8 weeks, the game needs either a content update schedule or a density reduction to sustain D60+ retention, and content updates at that cadence are a v0 cost commitment the brief explicitly wants to minimize.
- **P2**: Lure-reuse pressure creates implicit consumption treadmill
  - When: If encounter outcomes are strongly tied to lure novelty (new lures = new Unc families, repeated lures = same Unc families), players who exhaust their media library face implicit pressure to consume new media to unlock new encounters — this is a soft microtransaction substitute that conflicts with the no-treadmill design principle.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
