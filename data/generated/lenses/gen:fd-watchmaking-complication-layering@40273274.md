## Review Approach

### 1. Primary indications

- Primary indications — what is the equivalent of hour-and-minute hands (the always-visible top-level read), and is it protected from sub-dial clutter?

### 2. Complication layering order

- Complication layering order — which DB is the foreground (Initiatives, per PRD), which is mid-ground (Projects), which is back-plate (Sessions)? Are these roles declared?

### 3. Sub-dial discipline

- Sub-dial discipline — when a property is drill-down-only, is it kept off the default view, or does it leak into the primary face?

### 4. Hand collision

- Hand collision — do any two fields encode overlapping concepts (e.g., status on a Project AND derived rollup status from Initiatives) such that the two 'hands' point at conflicting times?

### 5. Power-reserve indication

- Power-reserve indication — is there a visible signal of substrate health (sync lag, last successful run) so the operator knows the watch is wound?

### 6. Reversibility of complications

- Reversibility of complications — can a new field be removed cleanly, or does each addition lock in an irreversible movement modification?
