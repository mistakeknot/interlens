## Task Context

The game runs over extended time periods where elves age and develop. Personality should mature, not just persist — becoming more defined rather than more averaged.

## Review Approach

### 1. Evaluate the 'nebari' principle: a bonsai's character sta...

- Evaluate the 'nebari' principle: a bonsai's character starts at the root (base). What is the 'root' of an elf's personality — the foundational trait that everything else grows from? Is 3 axes 3 roots, or should there be a primary trait that shapes how the others express?

### 2. For 'jin

- Check for 'jin and shari' (deadwood features): bonsai artists sometimes expose dead wood to show the tree's struggle history. Does the personality system allow for visible 'scars' — personality features that resulted from difficult experiences?

### 3. Assess the 'time

- Assess the 'time and patience' dimension: bonsai character develops over decades of subtle intervention. Does the system support personality MATURATION — not just drift, but deepening? An introvert becoming more nuanced in their introversion, not just more or less introverted?

### 4. Evaluate whether personality has 'growth direction'

- Evaluate whether personality has 'growth direction' — bonsai artists work WITH the tree's natural growth tendency, not against it. Can the curator work WITH an elf's personality rather than trying to change it?

### 5. For 'negative space' (ma): in bonsai

- Check for 'negative space' (ma): in bonsai, what you remove is as important as what remains. Does the personality system make ABSENCE meaningful — what an elf lacks defines them as much as what they have?

## Severity Calibration

- **P2**: Personality is fully expressed at spawn and never deepens — like a bonsai that arrives pre-styled with no room for the artist's intervention or the tree's growth
  - When: Personality values are set at creation and only change through random drift
- **P3**: No primary character trait — all 3 axes are equally weighted, so no elf has a 'defining' personality feature that the player remembers them by
  - When: All axes have equal behavioral impact regardless of their relative values

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
