## Decision Lens

Every sequential process with cumulative state evaluated as a lacquer layering problem: does each step have clear completion criteria before the next step begins? Does the state machine forbid transitions that would compromise previous work? Is the evolving quality tracked per-step with indicators that distinguish real degradation from measurement noise? The nuri-shi's discipline is that there is no 'good enough' cure — a layer is either fully cured and ready for the next coat, or it is not, and building on an inadequately cured layer is not a recoverable error. - P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
