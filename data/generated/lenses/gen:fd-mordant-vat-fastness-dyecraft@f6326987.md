## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Every generated position must be coerced to one of fourteen PolicyStance variants before it can touch the worldview at all, because record_choice takes a PolicyStance and closest_stance always returns the nearest match once five choices exist. 'Ban assault dogs and price-control burritos' is shade-matched to Regulate, and the axis EMA moves as though the player had endorsed generic technology regulation. The match never fails, so nothing anywhere reports that a position was off-card; the demonstrated worldview is a projection of a projection, and the more heterodox the world's discourse becomes the more confidently the reader misfiles it. The dyer's own answer is that a shade with no close swatch is recorded as a NEW SAMPLE, and the card grows.
  - When: When the worldview or ★ reader consumes positions only through nearest-match onto a fixed small stance set with no off-card outcome and no path for the set to grow from what the world produced.
- **P0**: A position's political colour is fixed at the moment of adoption and never re-read after the world has seen it. The player adopts the burrito price-control plank believing it reads as anti-corporate solidarity; in the epistemic regime it lands in, it airs as frivolous particularism — and nothing in the pipeline ever notices, because the colour was recorded in the vat. The one confrontation the feature exists to stage (what you believe you did versus what you demonstrably did) cannot occur, since the demonstrated side is computed from the intention side.
  - When: When the worldview estimate is updated from the stance at choice time rather than from what the position turned into on public exposure, with no lag and no possible divergence between the two.
- **P1**: Every position takes at the same rate and fades at the same rate — one uniform learning constant, no fixation step, no fastness grades. A position the player has defended publicly six times through hostile scrutiny and a throwaway one adopted once dye to the same depth and decay identically; unfixed positions never bleed onto their neighbours, so nothing explains why fragments bundle; and the whole corpus is uniformly petit teint, which means nothing in the world can be durable enough to be worth suppressing. Maximum expressiveness produces a field in which no position is more permanent than any other.
  - When: When persistence is a single uniform decay applied to all positions, with no fixation operation, no separate light/wash/rub fastness grades, and no consequence for unfixed positions touching their neighbours.
- **P2**: Suppression is one verb. Blocking a position from taking hold in a public before it lands — cheap, removable, and leaving the public entirely undamaged for later — is charged and resolved identically to bleaching a position out of a public that already holds it, which in the craft costs far more and permanently weakens the fibre. The player is therefore never made to choose between arriving early with a resist and arriving late with a discharge, and suppression carries no collateral consequence for the public it is applied to.
  - When: When the suppression verb does not distinguish pre-uptake blocking (reversible, no damage to the bearer) from post-uptake removal (irreversible, damages the bearer).

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
