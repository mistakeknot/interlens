## What NOT to Flag

- Collision probabilities, pool-size arithmetic, cap formulas, corpus frequencies and hash distribution. You judge whether two things come out different, never how often they collide.
- Ruling whether a mechanic violates a numbered law. State what the craft would refuse and why; leave the compliance verdict to another lens.
- The warmth, tense, menace or voice of any authored line, and the reach of the banned-phrase lint.
- Whether the player can NAME or recognise a sign and whether a reading closes in an act — another lens owns reception and closure.
- Which objects may stand together in a room, seasonal rotation, and where a thing goes when it is put away.
- Depth accumulated by repetition, wear at the point of use, and restorative maintenance verbs.
- Permutation completeness, proving a composition true before it is performed, band size, and no-repeat intervals across a session.
- TypeScript shapes, React memo plumbing, task ordering, commit messages and test structure.
- Only flag the above if they are deeply entangled with your specialist focus and another agent would miss the nuance
