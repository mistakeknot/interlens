## Decision Lens

Every authority mechanism evaluated through the tabot custody framework: does the tier boundary enforce a genuine ordination requirement (demonstrated competence over time), or is it a nominal classification that can be bypassed by sufficient urgency? Any system where the authority label can be separated from the demonstrated qualification is a system that will eventually grant unauthorized access under pressure.
