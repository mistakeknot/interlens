## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The evening is recomputed rather than recorded. T5 makes movieNightFor pure with no storage, and T10 derives last night by calling it again with dateKeyFor(nowMs - 86400000) and the CURRENT regularsWithAesthetics list. So the morning account of last night is assembled from today's roster: an Unc promoted to regular overnight is reported as having attended a night before he was one; a nickname changed this morning rewrites who was on the porch last night; an Unc who dropped out of the regulars list vanishes from an evening the player watched. The plan's own architecture line claims 'live and morning-after renders both derive it' as a purity win, and the absence-as-content clause promises 'nothing is lost by missing it live' — but the thing the player comes back for, per decision 1, is precisely who came by, and that is the field being recomputed.
  - When: P0 whenever the roster can change between night and morning, which is every promotion, every nickname edit, and every tenure decay. It is not fixed by more determinism: the function is already deterministic in its inputs, and the inputs are what move. The smallest repair is to persist the derived guest list and names at the moment the night is first rendered, in the persisted-at-mint idiom the plan already cites from cx4.
- **P1**: A verb the ruled constraints require to be reversible ships with no way back. The Ruled Constraints section states that all new verbs are 'positive-only, additive, reversible', and the brainstorm's decision 3 admits player verbs on exactly that basis. T7 then specifies addFieldNote with no delete and no edit, defended as 'notes are a journal, not a wiki', with FIELD_NOTE_SLOTS giving tier visited a single slot. A player's first note on the first old man they met — a typo, a joke that curdles, a line about the wrong Unc — occupies his only slot permanently and renders on his card beside authored content until tenure grows. The tradition's answer is not undo but a ruling stage: the verse is offered, judged, and only then written.
  - When: P1 as written, because the note is the player's own voice and the harm is confined to one card. Escalates if field notes ever feed anything mechanical. Note that a confirm-before-write step satisfies the constraint's spirit without turning the journal into a wiki, and that open question 2 explicitly left 'journal register guardrails' unresolved, so this is an unclosed design call being implemented rather than a detail.
- **P2**: The no-menace lint is a gate that never fires. BANNED_PHRASES lists leaving, leave you, gone for good, last chance, missed your, won't be back, disappointed, should have, too late — and no line in the 24 symbols or the 24 takes, 6 exchanges, and 6 moments contains any of them, nor would a writer working in the stated register naturally produce one. Meanwhile the list is a case-insensitive substring match, so an ordinary cozy line about a porch light left on for someone, or a guest who should have known better about the third act, trips it, while a genuinely cold line — an Unc noting who was not there — passes clean. The plan then leans on this list twice: the data tests in T1/T2 and the determinism sweep in T8, and acceptance criterion 8 describes it as 'register lint is enforced by data tests'.
  - When: P2 because nothing currently in the pools is menacing, so the null gate costs nothing today; it becomes P1 the moment authored content grows without mk reading every line, since the plan cites the lint as the thing that makes growth 'data-only' and safe. The tradition's fix is an interval and successor law, not a longer word list.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
