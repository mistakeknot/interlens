## Decision Lens

Collaboration health cannot be measured by a single metric or captured in a status page. It is read through subtle signals — the rhythm of proposals and annotations, the balance between agent breadth and human depth, the temperature of governance discussions. Different participants have different constitutions (prakriti): some humans are deliberative (kapha), some are quick-acting (pitta); some agents are broad-scanning (vata), some are deep-focused. The system should honor these constitutions rather than forcing uniform engagement patterns. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
