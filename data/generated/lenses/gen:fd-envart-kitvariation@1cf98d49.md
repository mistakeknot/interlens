  - When: triggers on any room whose material word maps to the wash, metal, or streak family -- a majority-covering slice of the six-family table, not an edge case
- **P1**: the AO atlas is baked once per piece under one reference lighting/albedo condition and then reused under every family and every compiled room tint; a bake tuned for ashlar's neutral gray will read as muddy once a strongly saturated compiled hue multiplies the albedo at 40%, since a fixed occlusion multiply darkens indiscriminately of the color it lands on.
