## Review Approach

### 1. Evaluate the Big Five vs proposed 3

- Evaluate the Big Five vs proposed 3-axis model: Big Five research shows which personality dimensions predict work behavior (Conscientiousness and Agreeableness strongest) — do the proposed axes map to these?

### 2. Whether axes predict different DOMAINS of behavior: intro...

- Check whether axes predict different DOMAINS of behavior: introversion→social, patience→task completion, pride→status-seeking — or do they overlap?

### 3. Assess 'person

- Assess 'person-environment fit' — I/O psych shows personality effects depend on context. Does a proud elf suffer more in a low-prestige role than a humble one?

### 4. Evaluate 'complementarity vs similarity' in team composit...

- Evaluate 'complementarity vs similarity' in team composition: research shows some traits work best when mixed (complementary) while others work best when matched (similar)

### 5. For the 'dark triad' problem: extremely high pride + low ...

- Check for the 'dark triad' problem: extremely high pride + low patience + high extroversion = toxic personality that should create specific negative dynamics, not just low numbers
