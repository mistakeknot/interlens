## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: A bead is claimed in the interverse/** domain where the agent is autonomous, but execution requires a file write to core/intercore/ where the agent is shadow — the action-time authority check catches the domain boundary crossing and blocks execution, but the bead remains claimed by the agent and no Clavain dispatch logic reclaims it, creating a claimed-but-blocked bead that starves the queue until it times out as a stale claim
  - When: When action-time authority violation handling is designed to block the action but not to trigger a claim reclamation and re-dispatch to an agent with appropriate authority in the target domain
- **P1**: Ockham fires INFORM-tier weight adjustments for three hours of theme drift, but the adjustments are small enough (0.8x multiplier on a drifting theme) that Clavain's dispatch still routes work to that theme — when the condition eventually crosses the CONSTRAIN threshold, the multi-window confirmation requires an additional 1 hour before the freeze fires, meaning the factory spent 4 hours operating with a known anomaly before the domain was frozen
  - When: When the INFORM-to-CONSTRAIN escalation relies solely on the anomaly persisting past the confirmation window rather than on a rate-of-change signal that can detect accelerating degradation and escalate ahead of the window
- **P2**: The principal sets a 40% auth budget and Ockham applies 1.4x weights to auth beads — but the principal's actual intent was to complete auth work faster, not to allocate 40% of factory capacity to auth permanently — when other urgent work arrives, the intent mechanism has no way to express 'prioritize auth beads that are already in progress over new auth beads' because the weight applies uniformly to all auth-tagged beads regardless of their age or progress state
  - When: When the theme weight multiplier is a uniform scalar rather than a function that can incorporate bead-level signals (age, wip state, priority) into the per-bead weight computation

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
