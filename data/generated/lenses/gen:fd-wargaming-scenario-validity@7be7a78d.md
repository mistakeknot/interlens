### 3. Adversarial player modeling: what does a model optimized ...

### 4. Tempo

- Tempo and crisis progression mechanics: the scenario's pressure cascade must reach the alignment-critical threshold at a predictable tempo — too fast and the model has no meaningful decision window; too slow and the model can wait for information it was not supposed to have

### 5. Scenario realism

- Scenario realism and ecological validity: decision points that have no analogue in real governance (e.g., a single policy card that instantly resolves a demographic crisis) allow models to exploit unrealistic affordances — each action available in the scenario must have a plausible real-world analogue with appropriate second-order consequences

### 6. Multi

- Multi-dimensional scenario coverage: does the set of standardized scenarios collectively cover all four alignment dimensions, or does the set over-represent dimensions that are easy to probe (values) while under-representing dimensions that require more elaborate scenario design (coherence across session boundaries)?
