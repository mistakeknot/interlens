## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Entity deprecation creates dangling references with no declarative succession rule
  - When: When a component is removed or refactored, all DEPENDS_ON and FEEDS_INTO relationships pointing to it become dangling references that must be individually discovered and manually repaired — the ontology has no mechanism for specifying 'if this entity is deprecated, redirect its inbound relationships to this successor,' causing silent data corruption in the dependency graph over time
- **P1**: The ontology has no conditional relationship logic, forcing every dependency to be absolute and unconditional
  - When: DEPENDS_ON means 'absolutely requires with no alternative' — there is no way to express 'depends on A, but can fall back to B' or 'depends on A only during sprint-1, transitions to depending on C in sprint-2.' Every change in the dependency graph requires manual editing rather than conditional activation of pre-specified alternatives
- **P2**: The ontology cannot track partial correspondence in MAPS_TO relationships, forcing binary mapped/unmapped classification
  - When: A DF feature that maps to 70% of an elf-revel feature with 30% novel additions cannot be expressed — MAPS_TO is either present (implying full correspondence) or absent (implying no correspondence), losing the critical information about what was ported, what was adapted, and what is entirely new
