## Task Context

Garden Salon uses append-only attribution records tracking token contributions per contributor per direction. The design envisions an evolutionary path where attribution may later support governance weight, revenue sharing, or reputation. Directions have a lifecycle from proposed through active to merged or abandoned.

## Review Approach

### 1. Intent capture in attribution records

- Does the attribution record capture intent as well as action — patent prosecution requires that the record show not just what was filed but why, because prosecution history estoppel binds the applicant to their stated reasoning; does Garden Salon's attribution record capture enough context to reconstruct the purpose of each contribution?

### 2. Tamper-evident direction lifecycle history

- Is the direction lifecycle history tamper-evident — can anyone verify that the sequence of state transitions (proposed, active, merged, abandoned) has not been reordered or omitted?

### 3. Abandoned direction history preservation

- When a direction is abandoned, does the record preserve the full history of work done under it, or can an Owner delete the direction and its history — in patent prosecution, abandoned applications remain in the record and can be used as prior art against future claims?

### 4. Direction continuation lineage

- Does the attribution model handle 'continuation' patterns — where a new direction builds on a previous direction's work — with explicit lineage, or does each direction start as if from nothing?

### 5. Schema sufficiency for future uses

- If attribution records are later used for revenue sharing or governance weight, are the records created today sufficient to support those future uses — or will the design need to retroactively enrich records that were created without those fields?

### 6. Priority date equivalent

- Is there a defined 'priority date' equivalent — when does attribution begin for a direction, and can a participant claim attribution for work done before the direction was formally proposed?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Abandoned direction history can be deleted
  - When: If an Owner can delete an abandoned direction and its attribution records, a participant who contributed to that direction loses their contribution history — worse, a bad-faith Owner could abandon and delete a direction to erase evidence of another participant's work before proposing a similar direction under their own name

- **P1**: Attribution records lack sufficient context for future dispute resolution
  - When: If attribution records contain only (contributor, token_count, direction_id, timestamp) without capturing the nature of the contribution (proposal, execution, review, revision), future disputes about governance weight or revenue sharing cannot be resolved from the record alone — the design must decide now what fields the record needs because append-only means you cannot retroactively add columns to historical rows

- **P2**: Direction lineage not modeled
  - When: If Direction B is conceptually a continuation of Direction A's work but the system treats them as independent, attribution for the combined effort is fragmented — a participant who contributed heavily to A but not B appears underrepresented in the final merged state
