## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Launch proceeds with no recorded demo artifact
  - When: Target audience responds to 'a working demo that shows a non-obvious capability' per the brief, and its absence blocks external attention entirely
- **P1**: Demo chosen is the 64-plugin interchart diagram or the architecture table
  - When: Breadth-demos fail the Tuesday-reproducibility test; viewer cannot reproduce any specific capability from the diagram
- **P2**: Demo shows a capability without the reproduction command on-screen
  - When: Skeptical viewer downgrades to 'cherry-picked' even if the capability is real

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
