## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The charter offers H0 as 'a publishable null if the manipulation and power checks succeed', but the design contains neither an expressibility floor on the base checkpoint nor a positive control at the confirmatory recipe. Nothing establishes that the untouched small open-weight base scores above floor on MenisBench and the discourse battery, and nothing establishes that the frozen token budget, schedule and replay ratio can install any measurable style-independent learned change at all. A null is therefore jointly consistent with 'oral–literate mixture has no effect', 'this stock could never bear this fruit', and 'this graft never took' — three findings the paper cannot separate, which makes the charter's headline publishable outcome unpublishable.
  - When: P0 only if neither check exists. If the base floor is required but no positive control is run at the confirmatory dose (or vice versa), this is P1, since one surviving check narrows the ambiguity to two readings.
- **P1**: The base checkpoint is fixed as 'an untrained reference, not a fourth training arm', and the corpus contract audits the treatment corpus exhaustively — rights, period, topic, transcription quality, duplication, affect-concept exposure — while requiring nothing at all about what the base was pretrained on. If that base already contains substantial oral-derived material, the oral-heavy arm reinforces an existing prior while the literate-heavy arm suppresses it, and the measured contrast is a reactivation asymmetry governed by the base's unknown composition rather than by the intervention. The published estimand would then be scion-shaped and stock-driven, and could reverse sign on a base with the opposite skew.
  - When: P1 rather than P0 because a documented base-composition statement plus one cheap indicator probe repairs it without touching arms, seeds, or budget. Escalate to P0 if the chosen base is known to publish no data composition at all and the charter still pitches H1 as a general claim.
- **P2**: All arms share 'an identical fixed replay component sampled from a base-distribution corpus', and the charter never states that corpus's own register or discourse-mode composition, nor requires it to be reported alongside the treatment mixtures. Being identical across arms guarantees equal quantity but not neutrality: a replay stream that is itself conversational or itself document-centred sits on one side of the very gradient under test and can consolidate differently against each arm.
  - When: P2 if the replay corpus composition is at least required to be manifested and digested somewhere in the corpus gate. Escalate to P1 if any manipulation-check or dose claim is computed on the treatment component alone while the model actually sees the mixed stream.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
