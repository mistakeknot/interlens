## Task Context

Brainstorm for elven colony sim. Small colony size (8-20 elves typical). Mentor eligibility: skill >= 7, student gap >= 5. One apprentice per mentor at a time. Graduation at mentor's skill level. School forms at 3+ graduates. Aesthetic drift toward mentor during apprenticeship. Existing systems: aspirations, satisfaction/departure, mood, lifecycle/aging.

## Review Approach

### 1. Mentor bottleneck under scarcity: in a 10-elf colony

- Mentor bottleneck under scarcity: in a 10-elf colony, only one elf may qualify as a mentor (skill >= 7); all aspiring students queue for the single mentor, creating a knowledge transfer bottleneck where nine elves are blocked on one — does the system model alternative paths (self-study, informal apprenticeship, aspiration redirection) for elves blocked from formal mentorship?

### 2. Skill ceiling clustering: graduation at mentor's level means the first generation

- Skill ceiling clustering: graduation at mentor's level means the first generation graduates at level 7-8, the second at 7-8 (training under first-gen graduates), and so on — skill levels cluster at the founding mentor's ceiling unless something breaks the ceiling; is there a ceiling-breaking mechanic or does the colony plateau?

### 3. Aesthetic monoculture from school dominance: a school with 5+ graduates all drifted

- Aesthetic monoculture from school dominance: a school with 5+ graduates all drifted toward the founding master's aesthetic center will dominate revel compositions; over time, the colony's cultural output converges on one aesthetic attractor; does the system model aesthetic counter-pressures (rebellion, generational drift, rival school formation) to prevent monoculture?

### 4. School formation threshold bottleneck: 3 graduates triggers school formation

- School formation threshold bottleneck: 3 graduates triggers school formation, but in a small colony the founding master may never graduate more than 2 students before their lifecycle ends — a system where most masters fall just short of the school threshold is a frustrated mechanics layer that generates no emergent schools

### 5. Multi-master competition dynamics: once two mentors exist

- Multi-master competition dynamics: once two mentors exist, do students have agency in choosing between them, or does the system assign based on proximity/availability? The choice mechanic determines whether healthy competition between schools emerges or whether a prestige hierarchy produces winner-take-all dynamics

### 6. Late-game convergence vs. divergence: after 1000 ticks with no external disruption

- Late-game convergence vs. divergence: after 1000 ticks with no external disruption, does the colony's aesthetic distribution widen (schools differentiating) or narrow (dominant school absorbing all)? The system needs a divergence force or the simulation reaches a fixed point that feels dead
