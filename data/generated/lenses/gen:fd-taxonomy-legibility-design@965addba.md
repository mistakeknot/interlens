## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The wing-set proposal ships as specified, with the five straw wings mapped 1:1 onto their listed jawnomicon families and no residual pool for the rest; roughly 60% of the eventual 645-creature roster (the 26 excluded families plus the ~99 creatures with no family membership at all) can never be favored by any wing roll, permanently starving most of decision 3's promised 'full jawnomicon bestiary' expansion of any legible wing-biased acquisition path.
  - When: wing pools are implemented as fixed 1:1 family-to-wing mappings covering only the five straw wings' families, with no catch-all or residual pool for excluded families.
- **P1**: Five is asserted as the literacy-appropriate wing count without checking it against roll cadence — if wings roll independently and uniformly at random once per delve, a player needs roughly 11 delves in expectation to have seen every wing once (coupon collector, n=5), a number nobody in the design computes or checks against how often the target session actually completes a delve.
  - When: plan-level open question #1 ('which wings, how many (3-5?)') ships without any exposure-cadence estimate tied to expected delve frequency.
- **P2**: The wing groupings borrow jawnomicon family names and members directly (e.g. 'Funerary' = Death-Criers + Underworld Court + Night-Comers) without checking that those families, built from motif/power-vocabulary clustering rather than tonal clustering, are actually tonally uniform inside themselves — producing hatches inside a wing that don't reliably read as tonally coherent to the player even though the family-membership mapping is internally consistent.
  - When: no cross-check is shown between a family's rationale/top_shared_vocab fields and the tonal label the wing design assigns to it.
