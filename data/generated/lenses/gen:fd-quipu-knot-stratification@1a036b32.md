The contributor guesses; subsequent compaction either preserves or destroys identity depending on the guess, silently
  - When: if F3's identity-constitutive event class is enumerated rather than predicated, and F4b consumes the enumeration rather than the predicate, every new event kind is a fresh judgement call that can retroactively erode existing Souls' continuity
- **P1**: F4b's 'summary' of warm events is specified by giving examples ('a sequence of routine facet-touches becomes a single tendency-summary') rather than by giving the inverse: what a summary must preserve so that the *omission* of detail is itself encoded. A khipu summary-knot carries the count and the operation; F4b's summaries may carry only the result, making it impossible for a successor reading only the warm tier to know whether what they are reading is one event or a thousand
  - When: if the summary format does not encode its own compression ratio and the operation that produced it, the warm tier is a one-way door that no successor can read accurately
