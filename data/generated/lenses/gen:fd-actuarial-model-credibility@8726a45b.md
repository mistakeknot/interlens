## Severity Calibration

- **critical**: Anchor suite constructed from lenses where at least one model has Z < 0.5
  - When: A model with high internal temperature or a long-context model with attention drift produces inconsistent lens selections — its low-Z outputs are averaged into the cross-model agreement metric, creating phantom consensus lenses that will fail regression tests when the model is updated
- **high**: No complement-of-credibility source defined; raw model agreement used as ground truth
  - When: When three models happen to share the same position-biased lens selection on a dilemma, the pipeline reads this as high agreement and promotes the lens — without a credibility complement, there is no check against the structural lens prior, and bias gets canonized as signal
- **medium**: Variance decomposition skipped; within-model noise treated as between-model disagreement
  - When: Re-running the same dilemma against the same model at temperature > 0 produces different lens selections — if the pipeline makes only one pass per model-dilemma pair, this noise is indistinguishable from genuine model disagreement, inflating the cross-model entropy metric

## Decision Lens

No single model's selection is credible on its own. Weight each model's lens choices by empirical credibility factor Z = n/(n+k), where n is response count and k is the credibility constant derived from cross-model variance. Blend with the complement (1-Z) times the overall lens prior. Flag any model where Z < 0.7 as under-credible and quarantine it from anchor suite construction.
