## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Encounters surprise the player rather than being expected by lure-frame
  - When: If option B's wildcard or option C's distance-gated multiples produce encounters the player did not anticipate from their lure choice, the etak frame collapses — the player has no way to calibrate their walks and the 'dignity of the open stretch' becomes 'when's the next surprise'
- **P1**: The kilometers between encounters are coded as dead time
  - When: If the UI, achievement system, or progress feedback treats the walk-without-encounter as low-value, the game has discarded the open-ocean axis and become a casino with steps; the open stretches must be valorized, not endured
- **P2**: No relationship between lure metadata and *when* the Unc appears on the walk
  - When: If a lure summons an Unc but the encounter triggers at a random GPS point on the walk, the wayfinder has no etak — there's no way to read 'we are 60% of the way to landfall' from the environment, and the calibration loop the player would otherwise develop never forms

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
