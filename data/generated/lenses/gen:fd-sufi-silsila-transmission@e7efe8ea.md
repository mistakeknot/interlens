## Review Approach

### 1. Chain integrity vs chain quality: in Sufi transmission

- Chain integrity vs chain quality: in Sufi transmission, a lineage that goes through a weak or problematic master remains valid if properly authorized, but a lineage that goes through an unrecognized teacher — however skilled — is spiritually void. Does the school's aesthetic lineage chain work the same way? A composition 'In the style of the Silverwood School' should derive its validity from chain integrity (unbroken graduation line) not just from aesthetic similarity to the founding master's work.

### 2. Hal vs 'ilm

- Hal vs 'ilm — state vs knowledge: Sufi masters transmit hal (spiritual state, affect) separately from 'ilm (doctrinal knowledge) — a student might acquire perfect doctrinal knowledge without receiving the transmitted state, producing a scholar but not a mystic. Does the apprenticeship model distinguish skill-level transfer (measurable, gradable) from aesthetic-orientation transfer (the felt sense of 'this is the right direction'), and can a student graduate with full skill but misaligned aesthetic state?

### 3. Ijaza and authorization to transmit: in silsila tradition

- Ijaza and authorization to transmit: in silsila tradition, receiving knowledge is not the same as being authorized to transmit it — a master explicitly grants ijaza (permission) to a student to teach. Does graduation automatically confer the right to become a mentor, or should there be a separate authorization gate that the mentor must grant (and can withhold)?

### 4. Multiple silsilas in one body: many Sufi masters held ijaza in multiple orders

- Multiple silsilas in one body: many Sufi masters held ijaza in multiple orders simultaneously, creating compound transmission identities. Does the system model a student who sequentially apprentices under two different masters from different schools, and does this produce a compound aesthetic identity or force a choice?

### 5. The absent master's presence: Naqshbandi silsila includes transmission through

- The absent master's presence: Naqshbandi silsila includes transmission through the spiritual presence of deceased masters — the chain continues through connection to the order's accumulated lineage even after the founding master's death. Does school membership continue to confer aesthetic orientation even after the founding master dies, or does school identity freeze at that moment?

### 6. Deviant branches and schism: many Sufi orders split over contested ijaza

- Deviant branches and schism: many Sufi orders split over contested ijaza — two students both claiming authorization from the same master, each denying the other's legitimacy. Does the system model competing claims to a school's lineage, or is school membership a flat set without schism mechanics?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Graduation automatically confers mentoring rights with no authorization gate
  - When: A newly graduated student can immediately accept apprentices — there is no mechanic for the mentor (or school) to grant or withhold teaching authorization, collapsing the distinction between 'has the knowledge' and 'is authorized to transmit it'; produces flat credential hierarchies where every graduate is a potential founder
- **P2**: Chain integrity is not tracked separately from chain quality
  - When: The lineage string 'trained by X, who trained by Y' is a display label but the system does not track whether each link was a valid completed graduation — a student who informally absorbed skill from a mentor who died mid-apprenticeship could be listed in the same lineage as one who completed formal graduation; chain break events leave no mark on downstream attributions
- **P3**: School membership is additive and uncontested after initial formation
  - When: Once a school has three graduates, membership is a permanent flat set — no mechanism for founding masters, senior graduates, or schools to contest the membership of a graduate whose aesthetic has diverged sharply from the school center; silsila tradition suggests contested authorization would be a recurring social drama

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
