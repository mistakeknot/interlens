## Decision Lens

Lift measurement is a commitment made before the experiment runs. Deciding the metric, baseline, held-out corpus, sample size, and analysis plan after seeing results is how researchers fool themselves. The MVP's credibility lives or dies on pre-registration. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
