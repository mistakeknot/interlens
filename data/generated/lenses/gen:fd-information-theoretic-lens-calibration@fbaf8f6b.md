## Task Context

The lens calibration pipeline is ultimately an information compression problem: 1,360 dilemmas x 12 models x 291 possible labels produces a high-dimensional data cube, and the pipeline's job is to compress this into an anchor suite that is maximally informative for evaluating lens selection quality. If the compression discards the information-rich disagreement cases while preserving redundant agreement cases, or if the lens library itself is internally redundant, Auraken will be calibrated against a low-information benchmark that cannot distinguish good from mediocre lens selection.

## Decision Lens

A calibration dataset is only useful if it maximally discriminates between good and poor lens selection decisions. Evaluates the anchor suite against the same criterion used in optimal experimental design: does each scenario in the anchor suite provide new information about lens applicability that is not already provided by other scenarios? A calibration suite of 100 mutually redundant easy cases is less valuable than 20 informationally diverse hard cases -- but the pipeline currently optimizes for agreement, not information content. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
