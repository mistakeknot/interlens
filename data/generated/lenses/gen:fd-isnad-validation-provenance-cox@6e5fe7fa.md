## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Executor and validator both report PASS from the same incomplete verify block, and the orchestrator lands a change that breaks a critical user or data-integrity path.
  - When: No independent criterion, alternate observation, or integration-level check can expose the common-mode defect.
- **P1**: The validator consumes frontier-model budget but cannot attribute its run to the exact plan, commit, environment, and criterion version it claims to validate.

- **P2**: The <=50% main-thread token-share gate improves while total tokens, latency, or retry cost rise enough to erase the operational benefit.
  - When: The pilot treats lane share as the objective instead of reporting total resource-to-outcome measures.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
