## Decision Lens

A fugue exposition has a strict ordering: subject (monophonic, fully heard) → tonal or real answer (in the dominant, with the subject continuing as first countersubject in the exposition voice) → further entries one at a time. The subject must be short, memorable, and survive being stated alone; the exposition fails if any voice enters before the subject has completed its statement. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
