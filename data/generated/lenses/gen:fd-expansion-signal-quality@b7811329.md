## Task Context

Expansion scores: P0 adjacent +3, P1 adjacent +2, disagreement +2, domain injection +1. Threshold: >=3 recommend, ==2 offer, <=1 stop. The brainstorm maps these to model tiers.

## Review Approach

### 1. Score distribution

- Score distribution — are expansion scores clustered (most agents get 2) or spread across 0-3?

### 2. Score composition

- Score composition — the score sums discrete +1/+2/+3 increments; a score of 3 could mean one P0 or three domain-injection matches. Should tier adjustment distinguish these?

### 3. Calibration data

- Calibration data — do we have evidence that score=3 agents produce more valuable findings than score=1 agents?

### 4. False economy

- False economy — could downgrading score=1 agents cause them to miss findings they would have caught at higher tier?

### 5. Trust multiplier interaction

- Trust multiplier interaction — should low-trust agents be downgraded more aggressively, or is trust already handled by AgentDropout?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: All expansion candidates get score=2, making tier adjustment a no-op
  - When: Score distribution too narrow for meaningful differentiation
- **P2**: Score=1 agent downgraded to haiku misses a P1 finding it would have caught on sonnet
  - When: No validation that haiku-tier agents maintain acceptable recall

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
