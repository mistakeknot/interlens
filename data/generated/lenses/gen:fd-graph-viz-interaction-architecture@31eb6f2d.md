## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: the design ships graph-as-hero with a full runtime force simulation of 400 nodes and thousands of edges with no fallback, and on first mobile visit the homepage becomes the single heaviest, jankiest page on the entire site — the exact failure the 4.1MB-bestiary constraint was trying to avoid, just moved into a different payload
- **P1**: build-time layout is chosen but the design has no incremental-layout strategy, so every export version reshuffles all 400 node positions — any user who bookmarks or screenshots a graph position, or any embed on Unc Rancher referencing 'the graph near X', breaks silently on the next data refresh
- **P2**: the slim JSON is sized only by counting nodes+positions and forgets that shipping edge evidence strings ('shares: drowns-the-unwary, water-spirit') for every one of 2,000+ edges reintroduces hundreds of KB of text payload back onto the homepage
