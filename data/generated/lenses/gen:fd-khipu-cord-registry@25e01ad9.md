Every symbol lands within a hair of every other symbol and at the exact centre of the being cloud; personality-neighbour queries for the 546 beings return a wall of symbols before they return any creature, and the mean that produced them is partly a function of them. The archive reconciles to a total that includes cords whose values were read off the total.

- **P1**: An archive tied with no keeper. 952 symbol entries are promoted with a vector, a graph node, and citations — and with gloss: null carried straight through from the registry, because nothing in the promotion path requires a gloss and no one owns writing them. The cords are arithmetically valid: validateCanon passes, the covariance gate passes, the site renders. But no consumer can say what any given symbol entry is about, the jawnsight grammar (which validates nothing about the payload it reads) derives player-visible traits from records with no readable referent, and the citation-stability rule means these entries can never be replaced, only amended. This is exactly the Spanish colonial inheritance: warehouses of perfect cords and nobody left who could read them.
  - When: When an option ships symbol records whose readability depends on a gloss, provenance note, or interpretation that no named person, script, or cadence is responsible for producing — and when nothing in the load path fails if it is missing.
- **P2**: Sums taken across group boundaries. The covariance report, the 47-family clustering, and relatedness V each continue to emit one figure over a corpus that has become two populations with different register coverage.
