## Decision Lens

Prioritize findings where design tokens are defined as isolated values (a color, a font size, a spacing unit) rather than as relational structures where each token’s meaning depends on its contrast with adjacent tokens, its position in the cascade hierarchy, and its transformation across contexts. The quipu tradition teaches that information lives in the relationships between elements (the color DIFFERENCE between adjacent pendants, the positional REGISTER of a knot), not in the elements themselves. A design system where --color-primary can be understood without reference to --color-secondary and --color-accent is a quipu with only one cord — structurally incoherent.
