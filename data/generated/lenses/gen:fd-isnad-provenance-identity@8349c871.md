## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Departure and deletion have no defined semantics against the spatial-memory contract
  - When: The launch gate is a specific cohort moving their group chat in, and the product's core promise is that places persist. atproto makes account deletion, PDS migration, and suspension ordinary events with community norms attached. If a departing participant's messages are removed, cooled regions and their summary cards lose the substrate beneath them and the canvas develops holes exactly where old context lives; if they are retained, the salon holds content against a PDS-level deletion its atproto-native users will notice. Either answer is defensible, but no answer means the decision is made accidentally by whatever the first implementation does, and it is a social contract with the design partner rather than a feature that can be added later.
- **P1**: Cached handles render as unverified identity claims
  - When: atproto handles are domain-backed and reassignable, and profile fields are mutable at the participant's own PDS. If the governance ledger, message headers, summary cards, or digests store a handle captured at write time, a later reassignment makes a past approval or a quoted line appear to come from whoever holds that handle now — impersonation with no attacker required. If instead everything resolves live at read time, the canvas becomes unreadable whenever a PDS is unreachable, on a product whose value proposition is that old context is always there. 'Schema otherwise intact' implicitly chooses caching without stating it or its consequence.
- **P1**: Summary cards without a named abridger become the group's memory
  - When: When a region cools, an agent proposes a condensation that sits at the region's center and becomes what zoomed-out viewers and returning members actually read; the full history beneath it is exactly the scroll-up excavation the product was built to eliminate, so in practice almost no one will open it. If the card does not visibly carry the authoring agent's identity, does not attribute the humans it paraphrases, and is not distinguishable from human messages at low zoom, an agent's paraphrase silently becomes the group's record of what was decided. This is the failure mode abridgment rules exist to prevent, and it compounds because later summaries condense earlier ones.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
