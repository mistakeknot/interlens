The organ builder's art is allocating the shared wind supply so every register speaks, no pipe is starved, and the pnigeus safety floor prevents catastrophic silence. ## Decision Lens

Every resource distribution mechanism evaluated as a wind allocation problem: does each consumer (pipe/agent) receive the pressure and volume appropriate to its size and function, or does the system assume uniform wind requirements? The hydraulis builder knows that giving a treble pipe the same wind as a bass pipe does not produce louder treble — it produces overblown shrieking. Proportional allocation means matching the resource to the consumer's requirements, not distributing equal shares. And the pnigeus ensures that even when the bellows operator tires, no pipe drops below the minimum speaking pressure — the safety floor is a physical invariant, not a policy decision.
