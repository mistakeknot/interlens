  - When: Soul::current_seq() = event_log.len() but SoulStore assigns SeqNos. If a consumer appends to an in-memory Soul and then persists, the SeqNos in the in-memory log will not match the SeqNos in the store. ## Decision Lens

The append-only log is the source of truth. Any design choice that allows derived state to diverge from a replay of the log is a correctness bug. Any design choice that makes the log impossible to migrate (vocabulary changes, renamed facets) is a maintenance trap. Correlation is not atomicity — confusing them produces subtle auditability holes.
