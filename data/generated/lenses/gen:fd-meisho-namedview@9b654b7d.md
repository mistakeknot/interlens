- **P0**: §4b states the coupling at biome and climate resolution — the most expensive and least legible resolution available. A continuous corpus→terrain function must stay coherent as corpus state moves, is exactly the cross-referencing burden that consumed URR, and at ASCII/tile resolution resolves to a recolored region that no player can attribute to any specific text. Meanwhile the doc's own payoff sentence describes a SITE — a blighted valley seen, then explained, then read — which is a bounded object with a viewing position, not a climate zone. The design has already chosen the cheap unit in its own illustrative example and has not noticed, so it is costing itself the expensive one for no stated gain.
- **P0**: The metaphysical/materialist/unreliable fork is presented as three mutually exclusive readings to be ruled between. It omits the path where the canon fixes an obligatory attribute of a place, inhabitants spend ordinary maintenance labor making the ground match it, and everyone afterwards reports that the place was always thus and the text merely noticed. That path is materialist in its physics and unreliable in its testimony simultaneously — the coupling is genuinely caused and the reported causation is inverted. Because the doc's table treats those as alternatives, whichever way mk rules will discard a mechanism the design needs, and the omission is invisible from inside the table.
- **P1**: The §4/§4b composition (invariant interiors, corpus-shaped exteriors) is proposed without any of the conditions that let an interior appropriate an exterior. Appropriated scenery is not 'inside plus outside visible': it requires a fixed station the viewer must occupy, an aperture that crops the view, a foreground deliberately suppressed so the distance reads as belonging, and a sight-line maintained against growth. A roguelike interior entered from arbitrary directions with a camera bolted to the player has none of the four, so the composition inherits the vocabulary of the technique without its mechanism and will read as two unrelated art budgets abutting.
- **P1**: §4b item 1 claims the environmental dependency recruits the player into reading rather than gating content behind reading — but the recruitment runs entirely on the player already holding the template. Someone who does not know the canonical attribute sees a valley, not a quotation; the causal mark is invisible as a mark. The doc supplies no first-hour path by which the canon enters the player's head before the landscape can pay it back, so the single strongest argument for the corpus-shapes-world model is asserted rather than mechanized, and it fails hardest at exactly the moment it must work — the first hour, before the player can read anything.
- **P2**: §5 selects co-present cultures because reception history requires co-presence and return, then never uses the cheapest dispute that co-presence affords: two traditions holding incompatible names and obligatory attributes for the SAME site, each maintaining it toward its own reading. That puts a live argument onto one already-generated location at zero additional terrain cost, and is the form of disagreement most legible at tile resolution, since it shows up as two names and two kinds of upkeep on one place rather than as two regions.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
