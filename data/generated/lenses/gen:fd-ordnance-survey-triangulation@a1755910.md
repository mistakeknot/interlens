## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Two of the five dimensions correlate r > 0.85 across the scripted baselines, reducing the effective Pareto surface to ~3D or ~4D
  - When: If the frontier's claimed 5-dimensional differentiation is actually lower-dimensional because dimensions are collinear, the headline claim is structurally overstated; surveyors would call this an 'under-determined figure' and refuse to close the triangulation.
- **P1**: No reference invariant is published against which future runs can calibrate
  - When: Without at least one stable calibration trace (e.g., a deterministic scripted baseline re-run across scoring-code versions), future ShadowBench versions cannot detect whether model-position shifts reflect model differences or instrument drift. Maskelyne's meridian exists for a reason.
- **P2**: Radar chart visualization uses normalized dimensions without publishing the normalization constants
  - When: If readers can't recover raw dimension scores from the chart, they are reading a projection without the projection's parameters — equivalent to an Ordnance Survey sheet with no grid north declaration.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
