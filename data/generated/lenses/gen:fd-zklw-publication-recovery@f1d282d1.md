## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A bad patrol deployment finishes after the revert deployment and becomes production again while the run remains recorded as reverted.
  - When: Rollback pushes a revert but does not verify deployment ordering and the reverted fingerprint as the final live state.
- **P1**: A tested local commit fails to push and permanently wedges the shared runner in a local-ahead state that future pull-only jobs cannot resolve.
  - When: The runbook documents manual repair but the next autonomous cycle has no explicit recovery state or safe retry path.
- **P1**: A reboot or long ingestion run causes the daily patrol to be missed indefinitely with no notification.
  - When: Cron is non-persistent, lock acquisition is nonblocking, and health or alerting only changes after a patrol process starts.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
