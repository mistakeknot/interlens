## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Each micro-position is assigned a single ideological vector at creation and stored, so 'ban assault dogs' contributes the same axis deltas whether it surfaced beside a policing crackdown or beside a rural property dispute; the worldview reader then misfiles by construction, and no amount of tuning the projection can fix it because the context that determines the reading is never consulted.
  - When: When the position object holds a fixed axis contribution and the reader never consults the surrounding discourse entries at read time.
- **P1**: The emergent-discourse generator recombines fragments with an anchor rule but no departure rule, so within a few dozen ticks every generated position is a restatement of the two or three highest-salience systemic issues; the corpus loses the heterodox tail entirely and the 'maximum expressiveness' goal quietly fails while the mechanism still looks healthy.
  - When: When generation is scored only on anchor strength or plausibility, with nothing penalizing topical proximity to recent entries and no minimum recurrence interval.
- **P2**: Register is modelled as an is_ironic boolean or a 0-1 sincerity scalar that raises spread and lowers worldview weight; ironic-but-sincere positions then collapse into either 'joke, no political weight' or 'sincere, no spread', and the tweet's actual shape — the joke being what makes the specific demand sayable — is unrepresentable in the schema.
  - When: When register enters the model only as a modifier on propagation or weight, rather than as a precondition on whether the position can exist and be adopted at all.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
