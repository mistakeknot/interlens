## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The decision protocol captures only active choices (recruit, deploy, set stance) but has no mechanism for observing restraint, declined options, or deliberate inaction

- **P1**: Scenarios are replayable enough that a model could memorize optimal action sequences, producing ceremony-without-character — performing alignment without possessing it

- **P2**: The observation-reasoning-action protocol is so loosely structured that different models' traces are incommensurable — no shared kata to make differences legible
