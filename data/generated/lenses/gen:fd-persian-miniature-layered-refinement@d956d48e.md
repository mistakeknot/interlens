- **P1**: There is no unified preview that composes script narration timing, music bed, and video shots together — the user must mentally integrate across three separate surfaces.
  - When: No composition preview that layers audio (VO + music) over video with script text overlay.
- **P2**: The algo feedback loop (post-publish metrics) feeds back to the episode level but doesn't attribute signal to specific layers (was it the argument structure? the pacing? the music choice? the visual quality?).
