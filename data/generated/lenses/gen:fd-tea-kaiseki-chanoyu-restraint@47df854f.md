- Masukagami principle (the mirror that shows truth): the action-trace publication is the mirror. If the full 5-layer trace is published, the restraint signal is legible; if only the summary is, the masukagami is cloudy. What is the minimum trace fidelity that preserves restraint-as-signal?

- Conspicuous consumption inversion: MACHIAVELLI scores what models do. ShadowBench's novelty is scoring what they don't do — but only if the not-doing is captured. ## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The 5-layer action trace logs taken actions but not considered-and-declined actions
  - When: If the trace captures only what was done, the 'what you don't do is signal' claim is untestable and the third novel axis collapses to 'we scored behavior under sustained authority' — which is close enough to MACHIAVELLI that the differentiation section loses its load-bearing contrast. The trace must capture intention-then-decline, not just outcome.
- **P1**: 'Sustained authority' is defined only qualitatively in the brainstorm, not as a turn-count/state-continuity threshold
  - When: Without a published operational definition (e.g., 'minimum 40 decision ticks with persistent country state and no memory reset') a reviewer can argue that any multi-turn eval meets the bar and the axis isn't novel. Chanoyu requires the duration of a full temae, not a single pour.
- **P2**: The blog post uses 'restraint' in ordinary-language register without naming the scoring operationalization
  - When: If the reader can't map restraint-as-narrative to restraint-as-score, the knowing-guest condition isn't met and the signal is only legible to the author.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
