### 3. Whether the taxonomy distinguishes between de jure rules ...

- Whether the taxonomy distinguishes between de jure rules (what the law says) and de facto power (who actually decides), especially in weak states

## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Policy changes in any domain take immediate effect without passing through a state-capacity filter
  - When: No implementation-quality modifier on policy execution
- **P1**: Informal institutions (clan networks, religious authority, military patronage) are invisible, making governance in ~100 countries unrecognizable
  - When: Domain 3 only models formal Western-style institutions

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

Governance is not a peer of economy and technology. It is the meta-system that determines whether any other system's outputs are realized. The taxonomy must capture this asymmetry or it will produce simulations where policy changes have immediate effect regardless of institutional quality. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
