## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A fresh executor follows every positive edit instruction and passes the frozen verify block, but changes an unstated protected contract or user ruling that the main thread had learned earlier; the final validator repeats the same outcome checks after the prior state and diagnostic evidence are gone.
  - When: The plan format has no explicit reserve or invariant section, the affected surface is consequential, and neither executor nor validator has a pre-transition check capable of detecting the leak before landing.
- **P1**: The first executor attempt leaves partial edits, generated artifacts, commits, or external effects, and the second strike starts from that altered state as though it were a clean repetition.
  - When: The two-strikes contract names neither restoration semantics nor a stage-specific resume point, so retry correctness depends on the accidental reversibility of the item.
- **P1**: Opus validation occurs only after the executor has completed and committed the whole item, although the likely defect was observable and locally repairable only at an intermediate boundary.
  - When: The validator's final replay can classify failure but cannot recover the evidence needed to attribute the failed stage or prevent repeated full-item rework.
- **P2**: A pilot passes the <=50% main-thread output-token gate while increasing the number of path-dependent transitions and the amount of protected state that must be reconstructed in plan files.
  - When: The pilot records lane token share but no stage count, rollback boundary, invariant coverage, or rework attributable to boundary leakage.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
