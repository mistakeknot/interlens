## Decision Lens

For every stage that touches captured material, ask: this raw can never be re-captured — is it being tended (protected from heat, labelled with age and state, watched for the window closing) or merely stored and hoped upon? And is our test for 'still good vs spoiled' a hard binary gate, or a soft threshold that lets a marginal batch slip into the good corpus? If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
