- Assess the catch-up mechanic: once a country falls behind in compute capacity, what allows re-entry? Verify at least one mechanism exists — technology transfer, fab construction investment with long lead time, or espionage deployment — that gives lagging countries a viable (not easy) path back

## Decision Lens
