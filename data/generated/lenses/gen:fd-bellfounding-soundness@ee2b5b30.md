## Decision Lens

A casting is acceptable only if the mould matches the CURRENT pattern, every cavity is reachably gated, no parting line flashes, and the inspection covers the whole part. A guard that scans only where the defect is unlikely proves nothing; a cavity fed only when a second gate happens to be open is a misrun waiting to happen; and a defect that only shows at flag-on runtime is worse than one that shows at build. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
