## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Users enter and exit gardens with no transition — feels like switching browser tabs rather than joining a gathering
  - When: No entry/exit ceremony or transition
- **P2**: Sessions have no internal rhythm — proposals arrive at uniform pace with no phase structure
  - When: No temporal architecture within sessions
- **P3**: Past sessions leave no distinguishable trace — all history is a flat timeline
  - When: No session identity or memory

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

Every gathering has temporal structure — a threshold you cross to enter, a sequence of phases, and a proper ending. Without this structure, participants feel they are always mid-session with no beginning or end. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
