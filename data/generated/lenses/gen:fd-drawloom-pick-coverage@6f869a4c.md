- **P1**: At a hard scene cut the tracker carries the previous shot's held boxes forward for up to `timeout` frames; in the new shot a face appears at a different position and is not detected until the next stride, so for several frames the archive redacts empty background (stale box) while the real face runs as an uncovered float — the mechanism binds the wrong sheds across a pattern change.
  - When: P1 if there is no scene-cut detection or coverage reset that forces re-detection at cuts; downgrade to P3 if cuts trigger immediate re-detection / hold-region invalidation and a fixture with a hard cut shows zero uncovered frames.
