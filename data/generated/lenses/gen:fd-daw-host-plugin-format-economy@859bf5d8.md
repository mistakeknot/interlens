## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A category gets absorbed (e.g., parallel-fleet orchestration) where the design space is still actively contested across plugins - the equivalent of bundling a stock compressor that freezes third-party compressor innovation for a decade
  - When: Plugin developer cohort exits the category; the host's stock primitive becomes the only option and stops improving
- **P1**: No stable parameter/automation contract - plugins cannot expose state/telemetry to the host primitive consistently, the way VST3 plugins expose parameter IDs
  - When: Each absorbed primitive reinvents its own plugin-state surface; cross-primitive composition stays ad-hoc
- **P2**: No host-mediated side-chain analog - plugins must invent their own IPC for composition (intermem to intersearch to interflux today)
  - When: Composition cost stays high even after absorption; the absorbed primitives don't compound

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
