## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A ranked IA/link-integrity skill reports a clean, complete map of a competitor while silently omitting that auth-gated and JS-only regions — often the actual product — were never reached, so synthesis and downstream CUJs are built on a chart that presents un-surveyed water as charted, corrupting every competitive conclusion.
  - When: A proposal reports coverage or link health without enumerating the site regions it failed to survey and the reason.
- **P1**: Page/route selection for a new site-wide skill dead-reckons from crawler happenstance instead of the site's own sitemap.xml/robots.txt/canonical lattice, so two runs against the same competitor sample different pages and the comparison matrix is non-reproducible.
  - When: A proposal does not anchor its route selection to the target's declared aids-to-navigation, making coverage nondeterministic across runs.
- **P2**: Artifacts blend instrument-measured facts with LLM-inferred or marketing-asserted claims without a provenance marker, letting an assumed depth (a claimed feature) be promoted to a charted one in the synthesis matrix.
  - When: A proposal's output does not distinguish charted (measured) from reported (assumed) water at the field level.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
