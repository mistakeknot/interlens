## Task Context

Expansion scoring adds discrete increments from different evidence types. The brainstorm maps the sum to a model tier in one step.

## Review Approach

### 1. Signal hierarchy

- Signal hierarchy — Polynesian navigators weight star paths > swell patterns > bird flights > cloud formations. Each signal type has intrinsic reliability. Expansion scores combine heterogeneous signals (P0, P1, disagreement, domain match) into one number. Does this collapse lose actionable nuance?

### 2. Attention vs. commitment

- Attention vs. commitment — weak signals don't change course, they change attention direction. Score=1 shouldn't necessarily downgrade (reduce commitment) — it might mean 'watch more carefully.' Is downgrade the right response to weak signals?

### 3. Multi-signal convergence

- Multi-signal convergence — navigators trust a reading only when multiple independent signals agree. Does cross-model dispatch account for signal independence, or could two correlated signals inflate the score?

### 4. Dead reckoning fallback

- Dead reckoning fallback — when all signals fail, navigators fall back to dead reckoning (known good position). What's the equivalent fallback when expansion scoring produces no signal?

### 5. Progressive commitment

- Progressive commitment — navigators make increasingly committed decisions as signals strengthen over time. The brainstorm makes one-shot tier decisions. Should there be progressive tier escalation?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Correlated signals inflate score: P0 in fd-architecture triggers +3, but fd-performance P1 is caused by the same root issue, adding +2 for a false score of 5
  - When: Expansion scoring double-counts correlated findings from adjacent agents
- **P2**: Weak signal downgrade when attention shift was the right response — agent needed higher capability to investigate, not lower
  - When: Score=1 maps to downgrade instead of 'investigate with caution'

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
