## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: The wizard asks for too many inputs before showing any value, exhausting the user's patience before the payoff
  - When: More than 2-3 steps required before any recommendation appears, with no intermediate feedback showing the selection is narrowing the field
- **P2**: Recommendation results appear instantly with no transition, making the output feel cheap despite the wizard buildup
  - When: The shift from 'I am choosing' to 'here is your answer' lacks any visual or temporal marking
- **P3**: Category tiles below the wizard use identical visual weight and rhythm as the wizard steps, creating attentional competition rather than hierarchy
  - When: User's eye oscillates between wizard and tiles without clear priority

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
