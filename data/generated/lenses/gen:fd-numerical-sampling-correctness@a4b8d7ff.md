  - When: Systematic floor truncation means samples just inside a shaded building footprint round to the sun pixel, biasing sun_fraction upward by up to one pixel width (1m) at every building edge. For short edges (8m alley, 2 samples) this can flip the majority vote.

  - When: A raster with a third value (e.g., 128 for 'partial shade' added by a future build_shadow version) would be silently excluded from total, making sun_fraction look higher than it is with no warning.
