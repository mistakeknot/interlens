## Decision Lens

Every specification-transfer evaluated as an iconometric problem: can two independent implementers produce identical output from the same specification, or does the specification leave room for interpretation that produces invisible divergence? The thangka painter knows that the most dangerous deviations are the ones that look correct — a face that is 11.8 sor-mo instead of 12 sor-mo looks fine to the untrained eye but fails the monk's measuring cord. Deterministic parity means the specification must be complete, unambiguous, and verifiable against reference exemplars.
