- **P2**: Path-level monotonicity not stated as an invariant
  - When: Unit tests assert edge-level monotonicity; A* operates on path sums. The brainstorm should commit to the path-level invariant explicitly or document why edge-level suffices (which it does for a non-negative weight function — but the rationale belongs in the docstring, not implicit).

  - When: Phase 2's UTCI weight will need its own coefficient; without a documented dimensional convention (e.g., 'k is the fraction of walking time added per unit sun exposure'), the two weights will not be comparable and a blended weight will have no principled scale.
