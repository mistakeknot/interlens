## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A bulk-import script loads fragments directly into the publishable artifact table, bypassing the sanitization and review gate entirely
  - When: Raw or unreviewed material with identifying attributes could be served to end users through the normal presentation layer
- **P1**: Fragment deletion removes the publishable record but leaves the sanitized and raw records intact and API-accessible to internal tooling
  - When: A takedown request cannot be fulfilled completely; residual data persists in earlier pipeline stages with no cascading cleanup
- **P2**: Redaction logs record which fields were removed but not which rule triggered the removal or whether a human reviewer overrode an automated decision
  - When: Audit trail is incomplete; future policy changes cannot be applied retroactively because provenance is ambiguous

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
