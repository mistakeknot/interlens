## Decision Lens

Flags matrix designs where a systematic evaluator preference (judge self-bias, position bias, verbosity bias) is indistinguishable from genuine quality differences between reconstructors, and where the statistical comparison method is insufficient to identify the pareto-optimal judge. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
