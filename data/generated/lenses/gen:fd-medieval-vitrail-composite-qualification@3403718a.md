## Task Context

Sylveste v5.0 replaces the linear L0-L4 autonomy ladder with a capability mesh of 10 independently-maturing subsystems. The mesh's thesis is that overall system autonomy equals the minimum of subsystem maturities. The vitrail lens tests whether this component-level view misses the critical failure mode of medieval glasswork: interface failure between healthy components. With 10 subsystems there are 45 potential pairwise interfaces, and the vision names evidence signals for components but not for their composition.

## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Unmonitored interface failure
  - When: The capability mesh tracks 10 subsystem health signals but zero interface health signals — Interweave reports 95% query hit rate and Ockham reports 98% gate pass rate, but the Interweave→Ockham interface silently drops 30% of entity resolutions because their schemas diverged, and no evidence signal detects this (like two perfect glass panels separated by cracked lead came that lets rain in)
- **P1**: Differential maturity stress
  - When: Routing and Governance mature to high trust while Ontology remains early-stage — the trust differential creates pressure to route decisions that require ontology context through the governance layer alone, bypassing the immature subsystem rather than waiting for it (like installing a finished window panel next to one still being fired, with thermal differential cracking the came between them)
- **P2**: No named structural substrate
  - When: The vision describes 10 subsystems and their evidence signals but never names the integration substrate that holds them together — it is implicitly 'the flywheel' but the flywheel is a process (evidence flow) not an architecture (iron armature), leaving the structural question of what prevents subsystem drift unanswered

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

Vitrail composite qualification — every panel is tested in isolation (color when backlit, stress under thermal cycling, lead came adhesion), but the critical failure mode is always at the interface: where lead meets glass, where iron armature meets stone tracery, where one panel's thermal expansion presses against its neighbor. The master verrier's art is not making perfect panels but designing the assembly so that independent component maturities compose into a resilient whole. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
