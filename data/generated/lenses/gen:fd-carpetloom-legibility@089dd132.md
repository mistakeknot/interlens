## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The design commits to full-text generation per artifact with no bounded repertoire of forms, schools, and physical marks behind it, so the 5,000 books have variety but no comparison set — a reader cannot tell a citation from a coincidence, a school from a one-off, or a forgery from an eccentric, and criticism, which is the product, has nothing to be about.
  - When: when any of the §6 dependencies (authentication, standing, environment) is retained, since each requires artifacts to be comparable to one another
- **P1**: Authentication is specified as a per-book forensic act the player must perform rather than as a delegated attribution carried by institutions and specialists, converting reading into a check the player must pass and putting a minigame between the player and the thing the project exists to simulate.
  - When: when authentication is kept as a dependency alongside a player verb of reading and publishing
- **P2**: The artifact's object layer — edition, hand, condition, marginalia, ownership marks, damage — is left ungenerated while prose generation is scaled up, so every book is equally pristine and the cheapest, most legible source of difference between two copies of the same text is unused.
  - When: when the design also wants provenance, forgery, or reception history to be visible to the player

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
