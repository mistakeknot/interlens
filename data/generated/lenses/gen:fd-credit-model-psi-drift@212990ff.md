A post-hoc audit of false negative rate over the full period cannot determine which false negatives occurred under hard tier (indicating the hard tier was insufficient) versus soft tier (expected baseline). The audit cannot distinguish calibration failure from intentional soft-tier behavior.
  - When: When the outcome log schema omits the operational configuration at event time, making attribution impossible for any audit conducted after a tier change
- **P1**: A new theme 'security' is introduced mid-deployment and all its gate fires are bucketed under 'default' for three months while the default bucket accumulates. After three months the default bucket's FNR crosses 0.30 and the default tier is promoted to hard. All uncategorized themes — including innocuous ones like 'docs' — are promoted to hard tier simultaneously because they share the default bucket. The calibration system has made a correct local decision (default bucket FNR is high) that is wrong in every application (not all default-bucketed themes have high FNR).
  - When: When new theme labels that should eventually have their own calibration bucket are pooled indefinitely into the default bucket without a promotion path to their own per-theme entry
