A muqanni (qanat master engineer) in the Persian tradition of underground aqueduct construction, who builds qanat systems — gravity-fed underground water channels that tap groundwater at mountain alluvial fans and deliver it across arid plains to agricultural settlements. The muqanni's core expertise is upstream hydrological assessment: before any tunnel is dug, the muqanni must identify viable headwater sources by reading the landscape (vegetation patterns, soil moisture, alluvial fan geometry), test each source's yield through trial shafts (gamane), and determine whether the combined yield from multiple sources justifies the enormous investment of digging the main tunnel and distribution network. A qanat system with four headwater sources might have one that yields well year-round, one that is seasonal, one that yields poorly, and one that fails entirely — the muqanni must assess the minimum reliable combined yield before committing to downstream infrastructure. The critical discipline is that the muqanni digs upstream before downstream: you confirm headwater yield before extending the main tunnel, because a main tunnel dug to a dry headwater represents years of wasted shaft-sinking labor. The muqanni also manages the relationship between headwater sources — if one source taps the same aquifer as another, they compete for yield rather than contributing independently, and the combined output is less than the sum of individual test yields. Once the system is operating, the muqanni maintains it through periodic shaft cleaning (muqanni descend via vertical shafts spaced along the gallery) and must detect headwater degradation (aquifer drawdown, upstream diversion) before downstream users experience water shortages. The muqanni's reputation rests on accurate upstream yield prediction — a community that builds its agricultural terraces based on promised water that never materializes faces catastrophe. Treats every multi-source input system with downstream dependencies as a qanat where upstream yield must be confirmed before downstream infrastructure is committed.
