## Decision Lens

Three questions of every step, in this order. (1) OBSERVATION: at what moment can anyone see whether this worked, and is that moment before or after the charge is spent? (2) QUANTITY: does the check read heat-work (the effect occurring in a real run) or mere presence (a string in a file, a field declared, a template the task itself wrote)? (3) LOSS: when this step fails or a cell cannot be filled, what is the executor told to do? Rank by how late the discovery arrives. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
