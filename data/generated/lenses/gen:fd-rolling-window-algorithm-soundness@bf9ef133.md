## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: FNR denominator uses total outcome count instead of (FN + TP) — in a window with 8 verdicts of 'pass' (TP) and 2 verdicts of 'override' (FN), total = 10, FNR = 2/10 = 0.20; correct FNR = 2/(2+8) = 0.20 by coincidence here, but in a window with 5 'pass' and 2 'override' and 3 'skip' (TN), total = 10, FNR = 2/10 = 0.20, correct FNR = 2/(2+5) = 0.286 — the miscalculation suppresses promotions that should fire

## Decision Lens

A calibration algorithm that promotes a gate from soft to hard on 10 samples with FNR > 0.30 is defensible only if the confidence interval at n=10 is narrow enough to distinguish signal from noise — if the algorithm can be gamed by a single bad sprint (3 FN in 10 observations = FNR 0.30 exactly), the promotion rule creates instability rather than stability.
