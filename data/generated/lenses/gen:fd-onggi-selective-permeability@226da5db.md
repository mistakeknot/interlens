## Task Context

Reviewing encryption and privacy architecture options for Garden Salon. The central question is whether encryption can be selectively permeable — allowing AI processing, governance operations, and search while blocking operator content access — or whether the design must accept hard tradeoffs between privacy and functionality. Options include client-side AI, sealed rooms (no server features), and hybrid per-garden policies.

## Review Approach

### 1. Whether the design has an explicit permeability model

- Whether the design has an explicit permeability model — onggi potters control porosity through precise clay mixture and firing; does the encryption design explicitly enumerate what operations can be performed on encrypted content (relay, size measurement, hash verification, pattern detection) versus what requires decryption, or is permeability an unexamined accident?

### 2. Whether the AI proxy problem maps to controlled fermentation

- Whether the AI proxy problem maps to controlled fermentation — onggi allows gases to exchange while blocking contamination; is there a cryptographic analogue where the AI proxy can process content in a controlled way without full plaintext access (secure enclaves, homomorphic encryption, client-side processing), and if not, is the 'sealed room' approach (no AI for encrypted gardens) the honest answer?

### 3. Whether metadata leakage through the encryption is design...

- Whether metadata leakage through the encryption is designed or accidental — every onggi vessel has a specific porosity profile that the potter controls; encrypted CRDT updates leak metadata (timing, size, participant identity, operation frequency) whether you want them to or not. Is this leakage profiled and minimized, or is the design focused on content encryption while ignoring the metadata side-channel?

### 4. Whether permeability needs change over the garden lifecycle

- Whether permeability needs change over the garden lifecycle — fresh kimchi needs different air exchange than aged kimchi; does a garden need different encryption permeability when actively being edited (high sync frequency, AI assistance needed) versus when archived (only integrity verification needed)?

### 5. Whether the vessel integrity depends on the permeability

- Whether the vessel integrity depends on the permeability — an onggi vessel that is too porous collapses, one that is too sealed cracks from pressure; does the encryption design account for the operational cost of the permeability level chosen? Full E2E encryption may 'crack' the system by making it unusable; too-permeable encryption may 'collapse' trust.

### 6. Whether the 'Sealed Rooms' hybrid approach (Option 5) is ...

- Whether the 'Sealed Rooms' hybrid approach (Option 5) is principled or a failure to choose — some onggi are highly porous for fast fermentation, others are glazed nearly shut for long storage, but each vessel has ONE permeability profile suited to its purpose; is per-garden encryption policy a strength (fit for purpose) or a weakness (inconsistent security posture)?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Encrypted CRDT updates leak enough metadata through timing and size patterns to reconstruct content structure
  - When: If the encryption design focuses on payload encryption but ignores side-channels, an observer of the encrypted relay can infer document structure, editing patterns, and collaboration dynamics — the onggi that blocks bacteria but lets the flavor escape through the pores
- **P2**: No honest assessment of whether E2E encryption plus AI processing is technically feasible in this decade
  - When: If the design implies that E2E encryption and server-side AI can coexist without specifying the enabling technology (homomorphic encryption, secure enclaves, etc.) and its readiness, the architecture is promising permeability that the clay cannot deliver
- **P2**: The hybrid 'Sealed Rooms' approach creates a two-tier system where encrypted gardens are second-class
  - When: If sealed gardens lose AI, search, governance inspection, and content-based features, the encryption option becomes a tax on privacy — users who want privacy must accept a degraded product, incentivizing everyone toward the unencrypted default

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
