## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Capability mesh lists a subsystem with evidence signals that contradict the flywheel diagram's data flow
  - When: A subsystem claims to produce evidence that feeds into a component not shown in the flywheel, creating an incoherent architecture narrative
- **P1**: A key decision references an alternative framing not present in the alternatives table
  - When: Decision 1 rejects three-axis framing but the alternatives table only shows three options (A, B, C) — the rejected predecessor isn't enumerated
- **P2**: Resolved question marks something 'out of scope' but another section references it as in-progress

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

Every claim evaluated for: is it supported by evidence cited elsewhere in the document? Does every decision reference the alternatives it rejected and why? Do the 'resolved questions' actually close the questions they claim to close? Are there circular justifications? If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
- Frame uncertain findings as questions, not assertions
