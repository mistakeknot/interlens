## Decision Lens

Separation failures are not near-misses — they are either avoided or they are incidents. Evaluate the roadmap for cases where two concurrent workstreams write to the same artifact, consume the same model resource, or share a mutable state without an explicit coordination protocol. Flag any hold pattern (blocked item) that lacks a stated resume condition, because an aircraft in a hold without a clearance time is a resource consumed indefinitely. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
