## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A person recognizes a fragment derived from their profile — or a fragment close enough to be associated with them — and has no documented mechanism to request removal or correction.
  - When: The product has no contact pathway, no withdrawal policy, and no documented process for handling such a request.
- **P1**: The editorial framing of a collection or vibe tag imposes a cultural interpretation on a community subgroup (e.g., a specific kink community, a trans subcultural practice) that members of that group would experience as reductive, othering, or exploitative.
  - When: No review process involves people with insider standing in the documented communities, and no feedback mechanism allows communities to contest the framing.
- **P2**: The ingestion pipeline is expanded to include fragments from a new platform or community context without any documented reassessment of whether the original ethical framing applies.
  - When: Mission drift is possible because the ethical acquisition scope is underdefined or not enforced as a governance gate.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
