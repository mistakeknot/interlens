## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Privacy claim has no linked policy for GDPR/CCPA-aware visitors
  - When: European visitors or privacy-conscious users will not convert without a privacy policy link; 'Email only. We never store your writing.' is a marketing claim, not a compliance signal — this may block a segment of the ICP entirely
- **P2**: Zero social proof for cold traffic
  - When: A visitor arriving from a paid ad or cold outreach has no signal that anyone else has validated this product; in the absence of proof, visitors apply their prior — that most SaaS waitlist pages are abandoned vaporware
- **P3**: 'We never store your writing' is an absolute claim
  - When: If the pipeline temporarily buffers text for processing, this claim is technically false; it should be scoped to 'We don't retain your writing after processing' to be defensible and precise

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
