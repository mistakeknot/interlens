## Decision Lens

A schema is judged not by its elegance at V1 but by the cost of its V7 migration. Object-first ontologies rot fastest at the edges — where an entity should have been a relationship, or a relationship should have been an entity. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
