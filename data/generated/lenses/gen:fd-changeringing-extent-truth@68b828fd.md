- **P1**: The row repeats on the first busy night. take = TAKE_POOLS[class][fnv('take:' + uncId + ':' + slug) % 4], with four lines per class and up to four guests drawing independently. The probability that all four differ is 4!/4^4 = 24/256, so 90.6 percent of four-guest nights carry at least one duplicated take; at three guests it is 62.5 percent. Two different old men then deliver 'thinks the horse did the real acting.' at the same showing, on a porch whose entire promise is that these are particular guys with their own opinions. The determinism script cannot see it: it builds 8 synthetic regulars and asserts twice-identical output and cap compliance, never distinctness within a night.
  - When: P1 as written. Escalates toward P0 if the morning-after path also prints duplicated takes back to back in morningLines, which is where the duplication is most visible. The fix is arithmetic rather than structural — either draw without replacement inside a night, or size the pools so 4 guests collide rarely — so grade it as a pool-size defect, not a redesign.
