## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Gate variant: a subsystem files N-1 Break receipts across its Compound window and one high-severity receipt at day 89 of a 90-day window, passing the gate. The late burst was triggered by an external audit, not autonomous self-observation.
  - When: The gate passes a subsystem whose self-observation was effectively absent for most of Compound; the jo-ha-kyū analogy is violated — the break was not organic to the build but a compliance artifact filed to clear the gate.
- **P1**: Continuous-mode variant: the receipt rate threshold is set without reference to Compound window length, so a long Compound window (due to slow evidence accumulation) requires a rate that is arithmetically impossible to maintain for its full duration given the volume of operations.
  - When: Continuous mode creates an unachievable rate floor for slow-maturing subsystems, forcing either an architectural exception or artificially shortened Compound windows that undermine evidence quality.
- **P2**: Gate variant: N is a fixed integer not indexed to Compound window duration or subsystem activity level. A high-throughput subsystem with 10,000 operations in its Compound window and a low-throughput one with 200 operations face the same N.
  - When: N is trivially achievable for high-throughput subsystems and proportionally burdensome for low-throughput ones; the gate becomes a tick-box for active subsystems and a genuine barrier for slower ones, skewing which subsystems advance to Epoch.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

In SRE, a burn-rate alert fires continuously when error-rate exceeds budget burn; a promotion gate fires once when the error budget is spent. The Break phase must choose: does ≥N receipts guarantee self-observation health (gate), or does receipt rate over the Compound window reveal observation quality (continuous)? Which failure mode — a subsystem that produces exactly N trivial receipts and stops — does the chosen design leave undetected?
