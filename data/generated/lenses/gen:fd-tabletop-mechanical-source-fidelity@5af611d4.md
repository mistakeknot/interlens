## Decision Lens

Every mechanic is a decision. If you can't describe the player's decision — what they know, what they choose, what they feel when they do it — the mechanic is not designed, it is described. Literary faithfulness in games is achieved through decision structure, not through naming. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
