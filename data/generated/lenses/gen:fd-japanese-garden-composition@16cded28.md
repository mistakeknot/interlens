## Review Approach

### 1. Ma (negative space)

- Ma (negative space) — does the layout breathe, or is every pixel filled?

### 2. Shakkei (borrowed scenery)

- Shakkei (borrowed scenery) — does the wiki borrow atmosphere from the game's world without recreating it?

### 3. Asymmetric balance

- Asymmetric balance — does the layout feel balanced without being symmetric?

### 4. Material honesty

- Material honesty — does the design acknowledge that it's a website, not pretend to be parchment?

### 5. Seasonal progression

- Seasonal progression — could the theme subtly shift with the wiki's content sections?

### 6. Stone placement (ishi

- Stone placement (ishi-wo tateru) — are the most important elements placed with the same deliberation as garden stones?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Every page is crammed with content, decoration, and navigation — no breathing room
  - When: Insufficient whitespace and padding
- **P2**: The design tries to literally look like a forest (leaf textures, bark backgrounds) instead of evoking one
  - When: Skeuomorphic decoration instead of atmospheric suggestion

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
