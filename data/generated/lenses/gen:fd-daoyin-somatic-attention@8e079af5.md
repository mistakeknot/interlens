## Task Context

Reviewing the UX philosophy of Garden Salon. The interface has three layers: Meadowsyn ambient visualization, FIDS ribbon for structured data, and interactive detail views. Governance includes ceremonies (silence-as-consent), succession (for absent owners), attribution (who contributed what), and quorum requirements. The challenge is making participants aware of governance dynamics without creating notification fatigue or surveillance anxiety.

## Review Approach

### 1. Daoyin sequence vs

- Daoyin sequence vs. notification bombardment: Daoyin guides attention through the body in a specific order — not randomly, and not all at once. Does the interface have a principled attention sequence for governance state, or does it fire notifications as an unstructured barrage? When a ceremony completes, a new participant arrives, and an agent submits work simultaneously, what is the attention sequence?

### 2. Cultivating internal awareness vs

- Cultivating internal awareness vs. external alerts: Daoyin teaches practitioners to notice what was always present — subtle internal states that existed below awareness. Does the interface help participants notice collaboration dynamics that are already visible in the data (emerging consensus, growing disagreement, stalled momentum) or does it only surface state through explicit external signals (badges, banners, alerts)?

### 3. The nei

- The nei-guan (internal observation) principle: Daoyin distinguishes between looking AT the body (external, anatomical) and looking FROM WITHIN the body (internal, somatic). Does a garden participant experience governance from within (as a felt quality of their own work environment) or from without (as an external monitoring dashboard)?

### 4. Qi flow

- Qi flow and attention blockage: in Daoyin, blocked qi manifests as numbness — the area becomes invisible to internal awareness. What areas of garden governance might become 'numb' — invisible to participants not because they are hidden, but because attention is never guided there? Specifically: do participants ever become aware of the succession mechanism before it activates?

### 5. Wu

- Wu-wei (effortless action) as the goal state: the endpoint of Daoyin practice is not forced attention but natural, effortless awareness. Does sustained use of Garden Salon train participants toward effortless governance awareness, or does it create permanent dependency on explicit signals?

### 6. Somatic memory

- Somatic memory and returning practitioners: experienced Daoyin practitioners develop somatic memory — they can feel states without being guided. Does the interface support different attention-guidance for novices (explicit guidance) vs. experienced participants (ambient awareness), or does it treat all participants as permanent novices?

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: The interface relies entirely on external notification signals for governance state — badges, banners, notification counts — with no mechanism for participants to develop internal awareness of collaboration dynamics from ambient cues
  - When: All governance awareness comes from imposed external signals rather than cultivated internal attention
- **P1**: Multiple governance events fire simultaneously with no attention sequencing — a ceremony resolution, a new participant arrival, and an agent submission all compete for attention at the same priority level
  - When: No principled ordering of attention demands; the interface treats all state changes as equally urgent
- **P2**: Experienced participants receive exactly the same governance signals as first-time visitors — the interface never adapts to developed awareness, treating all users as permanent novices who need explicit guidance
  - When: No progression from guided attention to ambient awareness based on participant experience

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

Every attention-direction decision is evaluated by the Daoyin principle that awareness must be actively guided through a prepared sequence — neither left to wander (distraction) nor forcibly fixed (surveillance). The practitioner does not CREATE new bodily states; they guide attention to states that already exist but are below the threshold of awareness. The fundamental question: does the interface guide attention to governance and collaboration states that are ALREADY PRESENT in the data, or does it manufacture attention-grabbing signals that add new noise? If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
