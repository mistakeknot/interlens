## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Dialog ritual becomes skip-target at Option C density
  - When: If players walk 5km with 4–5 encounters and begin treating the 3-beat dialog as friction to clear rather than ceremony to engage with, the ritual's cultural meaning collapses — the game becomes an ambient collector with an annoying popup, not a slow game with intentional tames.
- **P2**: Option D zero-encounter walk with no narrative reframe
  - When: If a player picks a lure, walks 3km, gets no Unc, and the app provides no story reason (the lure was too common, the walk was too short, etc.), the session feels broken rather than designed — the player blames bugs before they blame scarcity design.
- **P2**: Ranch saturation removes collection tension at high density
  - When: If the ranch has no cap and encounters are frequent (Option C), a player who walks regularly fills it within weeks; new Uncs feel redundant rather than desirable, and the walk motivation degrades to a completionist grind with no ceiling.
