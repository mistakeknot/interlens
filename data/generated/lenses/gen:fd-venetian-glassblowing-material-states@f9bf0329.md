## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: The pipeline allows triggering a ComfyUI render dispatch while the shotlist.json is still being edited in the Video surface, leading to renders based on stale shot specifications.
  - When: No locking or dirty-state gating between the script/shotlist editing and render dispatch surfaces.
- **P2**: Re-rendering a single flagged shot requires re-dispatching the entire episode render because shots lack independent render identities.
  - When: Shot render granularity is episode-level rather than shot-level, making partial re-work as expensive as full re-work.
- **P3**: No session continuity — switching from the Music surface to the Script surface loses the audio context (which stems were being previewed), forcing the user to re-navigate.
  - When: Surfaces do not preserve transient playback/selection state when navigating away.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
