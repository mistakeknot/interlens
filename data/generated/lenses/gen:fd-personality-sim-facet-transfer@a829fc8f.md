  - When: Shadow-work's archetype derivation weights are the product of gameplay calibration.   - When: If stress (a read-path derivation) feeds into evolution-rate (a write-path policy), then the evolution engine must compute stress before writing each facet shift event. This creates a performance-relevant dependency that is architecturally different from a pure derivation and requires explicit ordering in the simulation loop.
