## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The reader's key set is fixed and cannot be split. PolicyStance::Deregulate already serves both Labor and Technology from a single coordinate row, so labour deregulation and technology deregulation are one note; when the world's discourse generates 'ban assault dogs and price-control burritos', it is spelled onto whichever existing key is nearest and thereafter is that key forever. Every additional heterodox position adds another collision, and there is no operation anywhere in the design that says 'these two have diverged in the world, give them separate keys'. The misfiling is therefore not an accuracy problem that better coordinates would fix — it is structural, monotone in the world's expressiveness, and worst exactly where the game is most interesting.
  - When: When the reading grid has a fixed key set with no mechanism to detect that two positions sharing a spelling have diverged in the world, and no operation to split them into distinct keys afterwards.
- **P0**: The reading grid is also the storage format — positions exist only as coordinates on the five axes, or only as members of the stance enum. Frets have been cut into the only instrument in the room, so what falls between them is not merely unreadable but unrepresentable: the burrito plank cannot be held in the world at its own pitch and then read approximately, because there is no place to hold it. Every discussion of expressiveness then collapses into arguments about how many frets to cut, which is the archicembalo road, and the game's ceiling on heterodoxy becomes the size of an enum.
  - When: When the coarse readable grid used by the worldview and star pipeline is the same object as the representation positions live in, so unbounded micro-positions have no fretless carrier to exist on.
- **P1**: Everything is equal-tempered. One learning rate, one Euclidean metric, one uniform axis space, and the star selected by minimising total deviation. Every key is playable and every key sounds the same: a hyper-specific ironic-but-sincere plank and a bland technocratic one both read as a modest nudge on three axes, no region of the space reads sharply, and the option the star lands on is reliably the one that is a little wrong everywhere rather than the one that is exactly right on four axes and howling on the fifth. Meanwhile the narration layer is reaching for most_deviant_axis and finding nothing worth naming, because the selector has already filtered out every option with a wolf in it.
  - When: When the reading metric, the learning rate and the grid are uniform across the whole space, and the option-selection metric spreads error while the narration depends on locating a single sharp deviation.
- **P2**: Positions are coined in the world's tuning and read in the player's, with no transposition and no acknowledgement. Agents reason in economic / social / nationalist with a conviction weight; the player's demonstrated worldview lives on Collective-Market, Precaution-Transformation, Universalism-Particularism, Continuity-Disruption, Legibility-Opacity. A position generated by a movement in agent space is scored into player space by whatever ad hoc path the code happens to take, and the resulting error is indistinguishable from the player genuinely drifting. Two gamelans have been made to play together by pretending they share a tuning, and the beating is being reported as the player's character.
  - When: When positions originate in one ideology grid and are read in another with no stated transposition, no accounting of what the transposition loses, and no deliberate decision to surface the mismatch as a fact about the world rather than a measurement of the player.
- **P3**: The origin never moves. Every player begins at exactly [0.0; 5] and the centre of the political space is a compile-time constant that no amount of world discourse can shift, so a polity that has spent thirty years being dragged toward one pole still measures its players against the pitch standard of 2025. Nothing in covert politics is allowed to move the tuning fork, only to move actors relative to it — and the design has no ombak either: divergence between believed and demonstrated worldview is treated purely as a quantity to report and implicitly to minimise, rather than a beat rate with a pleasing range that the systems should be tuned to produce.
  - When: When the origin of the position space is fixed and unmovable by world state, and when believed-versus-demonstrated divergence is framed as error rather than as a tuned interval with a target range.
