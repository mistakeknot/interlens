## Task Context

Garden Salon positions itself in the gap between multiplayer tools (Figma, Notion) and agent tools (Claude Code, LangSmith). The wedge is Texturaize editorial collaboration. The governance brainstorm adds significant complexity (directions, attribution, evolutionary governance). The review target is the entire project's strategic coherence.

## Review Approach

### 1. Wedge validation
- Is Texturaize a strong enough wedge? One internal use case is not market validation.
- Does the editorial collaboration use case actually require all the infrastructure being built (CRDT, governance, attribution, Meadowsyn)?
- What's the minimum slice that delivers value to Ryan (the editor persona)?
- Is the roadmap front-loading infrastructure at the expense of user-facing value?

### 2. Persona coherence
- Ryan wants simplicity. The governance system (directions, attribution, snapshots) is complex. Is there a path from "Ryan sees track changes" to "Ryan understands directions"?
- The developer persona (sma) wants deep access. Is there tension between making the tool accessible to Ryan and powerful for sma?
- Agent-as-participant is novel. Is the product explaining this to users, or assuming they'll get it?

### 3. Competitive positioning
- Notion AI is identified as the most dangerous competitor. What specifically prevents Notion from adding agent-as-participant? Is the moat real?
- Claude Code / Cursor are adding multiplayer. Is the "developer-centric" framing of their limitation durable?
- Is the open-source core strategy viable? What exactly is open vs. paid?

### 4. Scope creep risk
- The governance brainstorm adds 4 epics of work (attribution, directions, governance, UX). Is this necessary for dogfood, or is it premature abstraction?
- "Evolutionary path: Emergent → Templates → Constitutions" — is this a product strategy or an excuse to defer decisions?
- The attribution system records everything but defers meaning to gardens. Is this a feature or an abdication?

### 5. Business model reality
- "Running agents on your projects is the natural premium tier." But BYOK means users bring their own keys. Where's the revenue?
- The proxy sits in the request path. Is this a feature (attribution) or a liability (latency, trust)?

## Severity Calibration

- **P0:** The wedge doesn't actually require the product being built (building the wrong thing)
- **P1:** Persona needs are contradictory and unresolved
- **P2:** Competitive moat is weaker than claimed
- **P3:** Business model assumptions need testing
