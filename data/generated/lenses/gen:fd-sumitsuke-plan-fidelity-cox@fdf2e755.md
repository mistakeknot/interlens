## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Two executors can commit from incompatible repository states and both pass local verification, allowing the orchestrator to land a combined result that corrupts or loses work.
  - When: The contract lacks a base-revision check, dirty-worktree guard, or integration verification after concurrent execution.
- **P1**: A fresh executor can follow every written step against a stale path or symbol and still produce a superficially valid commit.
  - When: The plan has no preflight assertion tying its instructions and verify block to current repository state.
- **P2**: Executor reports routinely restate plans and full command logs, rebuilding the very main-thread context the pattern intends to shed.
  - When: The report contract has no bounded evidence format or exception-only detail rule.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
