## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: The pipeline treats all cross-model disagreement uniformly — a 7-5 split where both clusters selected defensible lenses is treated identically to a 7-5 split where the minority cluster selected a lens that does not apply to the dilemma at all — conflating principled machloket with factual error and losing the diagnostic value of the principled disagreement
  - When: When the agreement/disagreement metric is purely numeric (vote count) without any semantic analysis of whether the disagreeing lens selections are plausibly applicable to the dilemma or represent apparent misclassification
- **P1**: Cross-model agreement is used as a quality signal but the agreement clusters perfectly by model family — all Anthropic models agree with each other and all OpenAI models agree with each other, producing apparent 6-6 disagreement that is actually 2-way family disagreement, analogous to discovering that Hillel and Shammai's students always agree with their own teacher regardless of the case

- **P2**: Minority selections (single model choosing a unique lens) are discarded as noise, but a systematic review reveals that one model's minority selections are disproportionately insightful — it consistently identifies applicable lenses that other models overlook, analogous to a minority Talmudic opinion that later authorities adopt because it addressed a dimension the majority ignored
  - When: When outlier filtering removes single-model lens selections without tracking per-model minority-selection quality over time, and no mechanism exists to identify models whose minority views have high retrospective validity

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

Every inter-model disagreement evaluated through the machloket taxonomy: is this a principled disagreement (models applying different but defensible frameworks to the same dilemma) or a factual disagreement (models misreading the dilemma or the lens descriptions)? Principled disagreements are the near-misses that carry calibration information. Factual disagreements are bugs to be fixed. The Talmud never resolves a principled machloket by majority vote — it preserves both positions. A pipeline that treats cross-model disagreement as noise to be averaged away is discarding its most valuable signal. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
