## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: There is no perambulation. The player's worldview is an exponential moving average updated silently on every card choice at a rate of 0.15, read through a coordinate table that was asserted by hand and has never been walked against anything. The record cannot be wrong, because nothing ever checks it: there is no cadence on which the player is made to walk their own boundary, no cost, no event, no moment at which the plat and the footsteps are laid against each other in front of the person bound by them. When the world's discourse starts generating hyper-specific planks, they are filed by the same silent arithmetic, and every misfiling compounds into the average unopposed. The demonstrated-versus-believed confrontation is therefore not a mechanism at all — it is a readout of a number that was computed by assuming the answer.
  - When: When no proposal provides a discrete, periodic, costly reconciliation of the asserted record against demonstrated behaviour, performed by the party bound by it, distinct from the continuous silent update.
- **P0**: The design admits exactly one kind of interest. Every card option resolves to one of fifteen PolicyStance rows, each with one coordinate row in stance_axis_position, and a position is a point. 'Ban assault dogs and price-control burritos' is not a competing claim to the same ground as energy policy — it is an easement running across several parcels and a profit taken out of one — but the only expressible interest is fee simple in a parcel, so it is crushed into whichever stance row is nearest and thereafter is that stance. The misfiling is not an accuracy failure that better coordinates would fix; it is the forced reduction of every kind of interest to the one kind the register can hold, and it gets structurally worse exactly as the world becomes more cross-cutting.
  - When: When the representation supports only one kind of claim over an issue, so cross-cutting positions must be expressed as competing positions on the same axes rather than as distinct simultaneous interests over the same ground.
- **P0**: Salience is stamped, not held. impacts.mediaAttention is an integer from 0 to 100 written on the issue file; it is an assertion about the parcel, owned by nobody, competing with nothing, exhausted by no one's use, and capped by no carrier's capacity. A newly generated burrito plank can be assigned attention without any other claim losing any, so nothing is displaced, no shortage ever occurs, and no dry year ever arrives to reveal who is senior. The plank therefore costs the polity nothing to hold, and a position that costs nothing is flavour by construction, however first-class the object model declares it to be. Nothing in the design can answer what happens when a hundred micro-positions and 353 systemic issues all want the ditch.
  - When: When salience is a per-object magnitude rather than a rivalrous stinted share held by a carrier, and no shortage rule exists for allocating it when demand exceeds supply.
- **P1**: A single current-state worldview table with no assurance fund. The proposal replaces tracing with a register: one authoritative structure says what the player's worldview is and what every live position is, indefeasible, no chain to walk. This is Torrens, and Torrens is defensible — but it was only ever solvent because the state budgeted real money in advance for the claims its register would wrong, and published the route by which a wronged claimant is compensated. The proposal budgets nothing. There is no acknowledged misfiling rate, no correction route, no way for a position that was filed wrongly to be re-filed, and no place where the cost of the guarantee is written down. Meanwhile the append-only ledger sealed on 2026-08-06 is a deeds system sitting underneath it, so the design now has both regimes and has not said which is senior.
  - When: When a derived current-state table is proposed as authoritative without a stated error budget, correction route, or declaration of seniority against the append-only log beneath it.
- **P1**: One displacement rule for every rate, so the avulsion is averaged away. WORLDVIEW_LEARNING_RATE is 0.15 for every choice alike. A player who has governed as a collectivist for forty choices and then, under a crisis, does something violently market-liberal has just had their river jump its channel: the interesting fact is that they are now standing on the far bank from their own boundary. Under a uniform 0.15 the event is 15% absorbed on the spot and gone within five more choices, indistinguishable from slow accretion. The design has no way to say 'the boundary stays in the old channel and you are now across the water from it', which is the single sharpest shadow-work moment available and the one the arithmetic is specifically constructed to erase.
  - When: When worldview displacement is governed by a single uniform update rule regardless of the rate or discontinuity of the change, with no distinct treatment for sudden departures from an established pattern.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
