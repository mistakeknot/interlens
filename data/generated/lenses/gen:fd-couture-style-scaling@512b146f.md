## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Style fidelity is evaluated only by automated metrics or user self-report, with no mechanism for artists to define and enforce their own style rules
  - When: If the artist cannot specify what 'in my style' means beyond the training corpus, the tool cannot reliably produce outputs that pass the artist's own quality gate
- **P1**: The product frames style consistency as a binary (in-style vs. out-of-style) rather than a multi-dimensional system the artist can tune
  - When: Couture designers manage style coherence across silhouette, proportion, surface treatment, and detail simultaneously — a one-dimensional style knob is insufficient for serious users
- **P2**: The MLP presents outputs as a set of equals for the artist to choose from, rather than as a draft the artist reviews against their own standards
  - When: The couture master reviews against an internalized standard, not by picking the best of a set — the framing of output presentation signals whether the tool respects the artist's authority
