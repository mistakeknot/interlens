- **P0**: A scaling rule runs out at a census extreme and the doc's response is a clamp where the craft requires a break, so a named and countable set of chambers assembles with a member whose modeled features are visibly out of proportion — a plinth compressed past reading, a cornice at the wrong height for the eye, a closing bay near zero width — and the defect is systematic rather than incidental because the extreme is a documented property of the compiler's output, not an edge case.
  - When: The arithmetic is run at the specific measured extremes from the build log, the count of affected chambers is stated, the visible symptom is described as a walker meets it, and the prescription says explicitly whether the fix is a break into a second scaled section, a refusal of the size band, or a compiler change that belongs to mk.
- **P1**: A chamber form is silently mitred: a rule branch claims it, produces something that assembles without error, and reads as a bug rather than as architecture — a form whose radius varies with height forced onto constant-radius tangent bays, a concave run assembled with bays facing the wrong way, a non-right corner given a right-angle corner post — and nothing in the doc records the compromise or its cost, so the build will ship it as though it were intended.
  - When: The form is named from the census with its instance count, the mechanism of the mismatch is stated geometrically, the visible symptom is described, and the finding says whether the honest remedy is a new piece, a per-form branch, or an explicit refusal of the form at v1.
- **P1**: The shop bake does not survive the room: the baked occlusion term is either invisible at the light levels the walk actually uses or compounds with the runtime lighting into a double-darkening, so the AO atlas costs a UV set, a bake stage, per-piece texture bytes and pipeline complexity for a contribution the walker cannot receive — and the plan contains no finishing pass in which the kit would be regulated against the room, only further work on the kit in the shop.
  - When: The argument uses the build log's measured luma bands and the register scaling rather than a general claim about AO, states what the term contributes at the specific luma of the station views, and prescribes either a concrete change to the bake, a runtime alternative with its cost, or a named finishing pass with a scope.
- **P2**: A variation axis is specified as a new rank where voicing would produce the same or greater perceived difference at no draw-call cost — a second modeled variant where a per-instance rotation, mirror, UV offset or tint jitter would separate the bays as well — so the kit pays mesh, bake and budget for a difference the instance buffer was already able to carry.
  - When: Both the rank version and the voicing version are described concretely, the perceived difference is argued at the distances and light levels the walk uses, and the costs are shown to favour voicing rather than merely asserted to.
- **P2**: A decision that belongs to the client by the trade's division is ruled in the doc or its build log as though it were a builder's call, so a taste question reaches the build already settled and mk never sees it — the ruling may even be the right one, but it was made by the wrong party and is now embedded in code.
