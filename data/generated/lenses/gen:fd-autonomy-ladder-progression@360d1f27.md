## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: Auto-merge tier reachable without rollback-rate gate
  - When: promotion criteria reference 'sufficient evidence' without a numeric threshold and no automated demotion exists
- **P1**: Global autonomy tier with no per-pillar scoping
  - When: kernel and apps share one autonomy level so a regression in apps relaxes kernel constraints
- **P2**: Override events not fed back into tier state
  - When: human interventions are logged but not consumed by the promotion/demotion engine

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
