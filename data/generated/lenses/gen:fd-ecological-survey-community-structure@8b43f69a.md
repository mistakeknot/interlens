## Decision Lens

A community detection result is only trustworthy when: (1) the implementation is verified against the reference implementation on the same graph with the same seed — not just checked for similar community counts, but for identical community membership for every node; (2) betweenness centrality scores are computed on the same graph representation (directed vs undirected, weighted vs unweighted) as the reference; (3) the result is stable across 10 independent runs with the same seed (some Louvain variants are pseudo-deterministic even with a fixed seed due to map iteration order). A custom implementation that produces '7 communities' without per-node membership verification has not been validated — it has been eyeballed.
