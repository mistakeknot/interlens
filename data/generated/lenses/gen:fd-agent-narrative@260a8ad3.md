## Review Approach

### Key Review Areas

- **Utility AI curve shapes and decision quality**: In agentmud, AI agents (architect, builder, evaluator, coordinator) make decisions about world content. Review whether agent pipeline configurations produce diverse, quality outputs. Check that evaluator scoring criteria create meaningful selection pressure — not just accepting everything or rejecting everything.

- **Storyteller tension/release rhythm**: Examine how agent-generated quests create narrative arcs. Check that dialogue trees have meaningful branching (not just flavor text leading to the same outcome). Verify that quest chains build toward satisfying conclusions rather than abruptly ending.

- **Event cooldown and clustering prevention**: Review how the governance system (proposal staging, voting) prevents change fatigue — too many world changes in rapid succession degrades player experience. Check that the pack evolution rate is bounded and that players have time to explore before the world shifts under them.

- **NPC behavior believability and variety**: In v0, NPCs use dialogue trees. Review whether dialogue options feel natural, whether conditions/predicates create interesting branching, and whether NPC responses account for player state (inventory, quest progress, flags). Flag NPCs that feel like vending machines (one interaction path, no state awareness).

- **Procedural narrative coherence constraints**: Check that agent-generated content (rooms, quests, NPCs, items) maintains internal consistency. Room descriptions should match their exits. Quest prerequisites should reference items/NPCs that actually exist. Dialogue should not reference entities from other packs or non-existent locations.
