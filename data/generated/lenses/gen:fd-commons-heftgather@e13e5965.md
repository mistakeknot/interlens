## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The upward channel (Decision 7) is routed onto institutional attention as its budget and its tarpit guardrail, while that budget is already saturated in code: INSTITUTION_ATTENTION_COST_BP = 3_000 against INSTITUTION_ATTENTION_REGEN_BP = 1_000 means one served request per institution per three weeks, with everything else deferring forever. In herding terms the common is already at its stint and the design proposes turning out a second herd on it while citing the stint as the reason it is safe. The failure is not that the channel is slow; it is that the guardrail and the channel are the same scarce thing, so the mechanism that is supposed to protect the city from resident spam is the mechanism residents must consume to reach the city at all, and neither the doc nor the code says which flock gets fed first. Overstinted ground does not fail gradually — it is fine, then it is bare, and it comes back on a clock nobody controls.
- **P0**: The identity substrate is keyed (ward, cohort, slot, seed), and both ward and cohort are properties a resident is temporarily IN rather than properties a resident IS — the sim's own conservation law moves residents between wards and cohorts every week. So the mark is not on the beast; it is the name of the field the beast happens to be standing in. Consequence: a resident displaced from ward 3 to ward 7 in week 40 either silently keeps a key that no longer describes them (the key is really slot+seed and two of its four components are decorative, which nothing in the design admits) or is re-derived under a new key, which means tier-0 reconciliation preserves the COUNT and loses the PERSON. Both outcomes break Decision 5's 'tier promotion hydrates the same identity slot (no mapping tables)' — the second requires exactly the mapping table it forbids. This is upstream of the whole LOD ladder and cheap to settle now, expensive to settle after Fork 1 writes a schema.
- **P1**: Decision 9 makes the reconciliation seam the design's central content and defers every rule about it to Fork 1, while Fork 4 separately defers who ticks what when. Priced as a gather, the seam has a shape the doc never considers: a whole-ward sweep is indivisible, needs all the state available at once, and cannot be interleaved with a live raven session — which is precisely the moment the doc says it happens. If instead promotion is a per-resident hydration, then residents were never really at large and the fog boundary is a rendering decision rather than a simulation boundary. The design currently reads as if both were true. Nothing decides between them, and the two have different save formats, different frame budgets, and different answers to Fork 5.
- **P1**: Decision 3 promises persistent residents with ties, arcs and being-Seen histories, but no property is named that a long-ticked resident has and a freshly-cast one does not. That is a restocked fell: right count, no heft. The commoner's test is blunt — if you cleared this hill tomorrow and bought in the same number of sheep, what exactly would be worse? If the answer is 'nothing a player could notice,' then the week-tick is a save-size cost with no product return, Fork 5's unprobed hole is being dug for nothing, and the honest design is tier 0 plus richer casting. If the answer names something real, that thing is the specification for the coarse tick and for the save, and it should be written down before Fork 1 chooses a schema — because heft is generational: you cannot add it later to a flock that never had it.
- **P2**: No pound is named. Residents that fail to reconcile at the seam, that are stranded by an unspecified demotion, or whose upward requests defer forever have no stated custom: no holding period, no forfeiture, no rule for what a resident becomes after a year unobserved. The existing deferral mechanism re-emits indefinitely, which is an infinite hold. This is a small, cheap rule to write and it prevents the specific late failure where a save accumulates a growing population of residents that are neither in the tally nor reachable — the wild beasts on the hill that every count disagrees about.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
