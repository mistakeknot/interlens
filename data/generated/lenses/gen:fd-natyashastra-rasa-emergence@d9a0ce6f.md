## Task Context

The brainstorm proposes 7 categories of attraction drivers as weighted additive scores. Rasa theory challenges the additive model: attraction is not the sum of drivers but an emergent aesthetic emotion that requires specific catalytic conditions to arise at all.

## Review Approach

### 1. Evaluate whether attraction is modeled as a PROPERTY OF E...

- Evaluate whether attraction is modeled as a PROPERTY OF ELVES (stored on the attracted entity) versus an EMERGENT QUALITY OF INTERACTION (computed from the meeting conditions). Rasa theory insists that shringara (erotic/romantic rasa) exists in neither performer nor audience but in their meeting — does the attraction system compute attraction at interaction-time from conditions, or store it as a static score?

### 2. For the 'vibhava' (determinant/catalyst) mechanism: rasa ...

- Check for the 'vibhava' (determinant/catalyst) mechanism: rasa theory distinguishes alambana-vibhava (the person who causes the emotion) from uddipana-vibhava (the environmental factors that enhance it). Does the attraction system distinguish between WHO triggers attraction and WHAT CONTEXT amplifies it? A revel setting (uddipana) should amplify attraction that a workshop setting would not.

### 3. Assess whether the system models 'sthayi

- Assess whether the system models 'sthayi-bhava' (dominant emotional state) as a prerequisite: Abhinavagupta argued that rasa only manifests when the viewer's dominant emotional disposition is compatible. Does attraction require a READINESS STATE — not just compatible personality but a receptive emotional condition (mood, recent experiences)?

### 4. Evaluate the 'sahridaya' (prepared

- Evaluate the 'sahridaya' (prepared-hearted viewer) concept: not everyone can experience rasa equally; it requires cultivation and aesthetic sensitivity. Should attraction QUALITY scale with an elf's aesthetic development? A more aesthetically sophisticated elf experiences deeper, more nuanced attraction.

### 5. For the 'rasa

- Check for the 'rasa-incompatibility' principle: certain rasas are incompatible (shringara/erotic cannot coexist with bibhatsa/disgust in the same scene). Does the system prevent attraction from forming when incompatible emotional states are active — fear, active rivalry, or disgust should BLOCK attraction computation, not merely reduce it?

## Severity Calibration

- **P2**: Attraction is computed and stored as a static pairwise score updated periodically, rather than computed dynamically from interaction conditions each time elves meet — this eliminates the possibility of 'the right moment' and makes attraction feel deterministic
  - When: Attraction score exists as persistent component data rather than being computed from current meeting conditions
- **P2**: No distinction between the catalytic conditions (WHO and WHERE/WHEN) — the system treats 'attracted to this elf' identically whether they met during a transcendent revel or while hauling timber, losing the most narratively rich dimension of attraction
  - When: Context (location, event, mood state) has no effect on attraction formation
- **P3**: All elves experience attraction with equal depth regardless of their aesthetic development — a newly spawned elf with no artistic experience falls in love with the same intensity as a centuries-old aesthete
  - When: No scaling of attraction quality or complexity with aesthetic sophistication

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
