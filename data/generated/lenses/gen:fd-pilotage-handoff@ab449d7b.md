## Decision Lens

A handoff is sound only when the receiving party can detect, from what was handed to it alone, that the picture has changed since the handoff was written — and when there is a named safe state it can return to at any point before completion. Judge each element of this design by asking what the fresh executor or validator can see, what it cannot see and cannot know it cannot see, and what it leaves behind when it stops. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
