## Decision Lens

An acceptance criterion is a gate, not a guideline. A gate that two reviewers can assess differently is not a gate — it is a negotiation deferred to close time. The migration-blockers PRD is gating the migration epic on four features closing; if any criterion is a vibes-check, the migration epic's open date depends on a subjective judgment that has not yet been made. The criterion that will be judged most subjectively is the one whose author was most confident at PRD-write time. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
