  - When: P2 when contained to one or two wings and repairable by re-cutting membership. Escalate to P1 if the same centroid drift holds across a majority of the wings, since the scheme's names then teach the wrong vocabulary system-wide.
- **P3**: Wing flavor text asserts tonal certainty over constituent clusters the source itself rates medium-confidence or documents as resemblance rather than genealogy, so the archive fiction claims a firmness the corpus does not carry and a player who learns the bin well will eventually feel the overreach.
  - When: P3 unless the low-confidence cluster is also the size-dominant member of its wing, in which case the whole bin's percept rests on a weak cluster and it is P1.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
