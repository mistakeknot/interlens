## Decision Lens

A slot that cannot air is not merely delayed — it displaces everything downstream. Evaluate dependency chains by their blast radius: which blocked item, if unblocked first, releases the most subsequent work? Flag any schedule where the critical path is unclear or where prerequisite completion is assumed rather than verified. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything

- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
