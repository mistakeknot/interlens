- Pure convention drift — inconsistent styling with no origin-claim consequence — belongs to fd-tratteggio-fill-legibility; typographic variance that changes no belief about a source is out of scope.
- Findability and nav-label prediction belong to fd-ia-wayfinding; a page a reader never reaches is not this lens's problem even if its marking is wrong.
