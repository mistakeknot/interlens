## Decision Lens

A proof of non-alteration is sound only if (a) the collation base is the true archetype, (b) every locus that could diverge is actually collated (not just a convenient few), and (c) the excised addition provably perturbed nothing it did not itself contain. Any gap in these three means the identity is asserted, not proven — and an asserted identity that a literal executor trusts is how a silent perturbation ships past the gate. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
