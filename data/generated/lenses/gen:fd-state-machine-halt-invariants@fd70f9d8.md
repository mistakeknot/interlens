## Decision Lens

A halt/resume system is a state machine. Every state must be explicitly defined, every transition must be guarded, and invariants must hold not just in stable states but during transitions. INV-8 is a safety invariant — it must be enforced at every entry point that modifies policy state, not just in the governor. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
