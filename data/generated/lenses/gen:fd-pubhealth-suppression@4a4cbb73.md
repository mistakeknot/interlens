## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A published collection is tagged with a combination of attributes (e.g., 'nonbinary + over 60 + leather + specific US city') that plausibly describes fewer than 5 real people in the source population.
  - When: The combination is visible in the UI without any generalization, creating a de-facto pointer to a small number of identifiable individuals.
- **P1**: The tagging taxonomy includes a 'rare hobby' or 'unusual occupation' signal that, when combined with age range, creates cells of size 1-3 across most geographic regions.
  - When: No minimum-cell-size check is documented in the anonymization transform or the editorial review workflow.
- **P2**: Sequential collection updates allow a reader to infer which fragment was added in a given editorial cycle, reducing the anonymizing crowd to a single new entry.
  - When: The architecture does not batch-publish or timestamp-blur new additions.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
