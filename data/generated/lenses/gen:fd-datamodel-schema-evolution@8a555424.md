## Decision Lens

For each variant, ask: what is the canonical representation a downstream consumer must branch on, and how many branches does correct code require? A schema that requires the reader to check `typeof x === 'string' ? [x] : x` at every call site is a design bug wearing a type signature. A `.default([])` is a policy decision ('absence means empty') smuggled in as a parser convenience, and it must be judged as policy, not convenience. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
