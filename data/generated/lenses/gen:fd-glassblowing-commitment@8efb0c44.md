## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Selecting a use case in the wizard permanently filters the recommendation pool with no visible way to broaden or restart without page reload
  - When: The wizard treats the first selection as an irrevocable commitment when it should be the most exploratory step
- **P2**: The wizard's back button resets all subsequent selections rather than preserving them, punishing exploration
  - When: User who selected coding, then adjusted preferences, goes back to try research and loses all downstream choices
- **P2**: Category tiles and wizard lead to different model detail experiences, creating inconsistent commitment semantics across two parallel entry paths
  - When: Clicking a model from tiles goes directly to detail; clicking from wizard enters a comparison mode — user cannot predict the consequence of a click

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
