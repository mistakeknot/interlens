## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: The architecture has no enforced separation between the raw source ingestion store and the sanitized presentation-layer store — both are accessible from the same code paths.
  - When: A bug or misconfiguration could expose raw, un-anonymized fragments directly to the presentation layer.
- **P1**: There is no documented ethical acquisition framework specifying which platforms, communities, or profile types are in scope for ingestion and under what conditions.
  - When: The pipeline could ingest material from communities where the implicit norms would not support this use, with no governance mechanism to prevent it.
- **P2**: Published fragments cannot be traced back to the specific redaction decisions and conditions that authorized their publication.
  - When: If a subject later contests their fragment's inclusion, there is no audit trail to demonstrate what transformation was applied or why publication was authorized.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
