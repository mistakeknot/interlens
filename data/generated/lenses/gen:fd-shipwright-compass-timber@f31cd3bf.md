## Severity Calibration

- **P0**: A member is bought for a curve that is not in the stock. The pipeline commits a drafter to producing a 29-axis personality vector and kinship edges for every one of the fifty, but nothing at selection time reads whether a candidate's corpus contains conduct at all. n_sources counts how often a being is mentioned and canon_thinness counts how many entries its tradition already has; neither is a property of the material, and a gloss that is pure iconography or pure genealogy scores identically to one carrying a full narrative arc at the same source count. The defect is therefore discovered by the covariance gate, after the drafting cost has been paid — which is exactly how Ki, Mata, Raja Naga, Gnomes, Barnacle Goose, Sea-Hog and Sang Gadin were condemned, all seven for the same recorded reason: the sources preserve a name and a rank but no conduct. A batch of fifty containing several pure-morphology draws will spend its most expensive step learning what an end-grain read would have shown for free, the exclusion list grows one name at a time, and the defect stays unnamed so the next batch repeats it.
- **P1**: The bill of timber is filled with culls to make the number. Creature 26 is the largest quota and its class reached furthest down the pool to fill it, absorbing six candidates whose thinness key is a null read, while the small bills (object-being 1, fairy 4) closed early on whatever was nearest. A quota that must be filled converts a shortage of good stock into an order for bad stock — the greedy loop has no way to say 'there are only nineteen creatures in this pool worth converting' and stop. Because the six quota sizes have no stated derivation anywhere, there is no draught to check them against: it is not possible to tell whether 26 is what the bestiary structurally needs, what the pool happened to contain, or simply what was left after the other five numbers were chosen. Every future batch inherits the same bill and the same obligation to fill it.
- **P1**: Seven condemnations are recorded as a do-not-buy list rather than as an inspection rule. The named exclusions carry both the pieces and the diagnosis — a majority of scorable dimensions at confidence 0.3 or below, because the sources give a name and a rank and no conduct — which is a complete specification of a defect and therefore of a test. Screening those seven by name catches exactly seven rows and generalizes to nothing; the pool of 525 is never examined for the same defect, and the fifty drawn from it are never checked against the one failure mode the pipeline has actually measured. The yard is throwing away its own experience: each future gate failure will add one more name to the list and still no rule, so the cost of the defect is paid again every batch at full drafting price.
- **P2**: Green stock is worked and never re-surveyed. An entry that clears the covariance gate at draft time is never looked at again — nothing re-reads a merged entry against later harvests, later batches, or the siblings promoted after it. Members worked green pass inspection and open their seams in the second year: a thin-tradition entry drafted right at the bar will read as the weakest thing in its family once better-attested relatives arrive, and the only visible trace is an absence of kinship edges, which is indistinguishable from the kinship pass simply not having run. The bestiary accumulates marginal entries it has no mechanism to notice, and the diversity statistics keep counting them at full weight.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

- Whether the 29-axis eidogen schema and the covariance gate are the right standard is out of scope. Take the draught as given; ask only whether the stock makes the members it calls for.
