## Decision Lens

A field guide that covers '300 species' but silently omits the 20 most commonly confused ones has failed its reader at the moment of highest need. Explicit scope is a gift to the reader; implicit scope is a liability that compounds with every edition. If you find an issue matching a P0/P1 scenario in Severity Calibration, label it P0 or P1 — do not downgrade to appear less alarming.
