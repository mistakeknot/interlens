## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: All attraction drivers are additive (weighted sum) with no interaction effects — like mixing glaze oxides by weight percentage alone, ignoring that iron + reduction = green while iron + oxidation = brown. Produces flat, predictable pairings with no emergent surprise.
  - When: Attraction score is a linear combination of independent driver values with no conditional or multiplicative terms
- **P2**: No mechanism for circumstantial conditions to transform latent compatibility into active attraction — like having the right glaze recipe but no kiln atmosphere to fire it. Two compatible elves who never share a catalyzing experience remain strangers.
  - When: Attraction accumulates from trait comparison alone without requiring activating events or contexts
- **P3**: All attraction is immediately legible at formation — no 'depth effect' where early chemistry is shallow and deeper compatibility reveals itself over time, like a celadon that looks flat until you hold it to the light
  - When: No mechanism for attraction depth to increase through sustained interaction beyond initial formation

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
