## Task Context

The brainstorm describes a research corpus that compounds with every episode and an interactive dialogue that produces artifacts. The open question about how Claude integration works — stateful, corpus-in-context — is precisely the reference interview + corpus scope problem.

## Review Approach

### 1. Is the corpus organized with any controlled vocabulary or tagging schema

- Is the corpus organized with any controlled vocabulary or tagging schema, or are research/ files a flat collection where findability degrades as volume grows?

### 2. When a dialogue transcript is added to the corpus as an artifact

- When a dialogue transcript is added to the corpus as an artifact, is it indexed and retrievable in the same way as source material, or does it become a dead file?

### 3. Is there a provenance record for each corpus document

- Is there a provenance record for each corpus document — when it was ingested, from what source, by what method (web search, YouTube transcription, paper extraction) — so that outdated sources can be identified and updated?

### 4. Does the research sidebar in the script surface retrieve by semantic relevance

- Does the research sidebar in the script surface retrieve by semantic relevance to the current paragraph, or by episode tag, or both? What happens when relevant sources span multiple episodes?

### 5. Is there a corpus health signal

- Is there a corpus health signal — percentage of documents that have never been retrieved, documents that are duplicates, documents with broken source links — visible to the user?

### 6. When Claude is used for the interactive dialogue

- When Claude is used for the interactive dialogue, is the corpus context bounded or unbounded? A library would have a 'scope note' defining what belongs in a collection; without this, the context window becomes the de facto scope limit.

## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: Dialogue transcripts are added to the corpus as raw text without metadata, title, or retrieval index.
  - When: After 20 episodes, the corpus contains hundreds of dialogue artifacts that are never surfaced by the research sidebar because they lack the metadata needed for retrieval — the corpus compounds in size but not in utility.
- **P2**: No deduplication mechanism when ingesting from web search — the same article can be ingested multiple times across episodes.
  - When: Corpus size grows faster than its informational content; retrieval returns duplicate results that erode trust in the system.
- **P3**: Research sidebar retrieval is per-episode scoped only, not cross-episode.
  - When: Insights from episode 3 that are directly relevant to episode 12 are invisible to the writer unless they manually navigate to the earlier episode's research folder.

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.

## Decision Lens

## Prioritization

- P0: Issues that block other work, cause data loss or corruption — drop everything
- P1: Issues required to exit the current quality gate
- P2: Issues that degrade quality or create maintenance burden
- P3: Improvements and polish — suggest but don't block on these
- For each P0/P1 finding, describe the concrete failure scenario: what breaks, under what conditions, and who is affected
- Always tie findings to specific files, functions, and line numbers
