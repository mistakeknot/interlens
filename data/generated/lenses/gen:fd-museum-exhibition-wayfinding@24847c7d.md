## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: No breadcrumb or section indicator — readers lose context in deep pages
  - When: Wiki pages lack positional context
- **P2**: All pages look identical — no visual distinction between getting-started and deep-reference

When in doubt: describe the failure scenario. If it wakes someone at 3 AM, it is P0/P1. If it degrades quality over weeks, it is P2.
