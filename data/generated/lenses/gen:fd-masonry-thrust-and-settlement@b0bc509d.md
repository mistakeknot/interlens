The tie is what fails, not either corpus — the ratchet reports drift in max |r|, redundant-pair census, and named-cluster membership with no way to distinguish settlement of the new work from a real change in the being corpus, and the baseline it compares against is itself the thing that moved.

- **P1**: The full lift is laid before the mortar cures: ~952 symbol rows are promoted in one pass under axis definitions that have never been exercised, and because citation-stable entries are never replaced, the definitions harden with the entries. When the symbol axes are later found to mean something different from the being axes of the same name, the corpus is permanently out of plumb and the only remedies are a schema weakening or a parallel un-citable collection.

- **P2**: A mitigation is added as unfounded mass: a symbol-specific schema, a second covariance gate, or a second relatedness baseline is proposed to absorb the thrust, but nothing states who stamps it, what it ratchets against, or when it is checked. The buttress becomes the failure — it silently stops gating while the design continues to claim the risk is carried.
