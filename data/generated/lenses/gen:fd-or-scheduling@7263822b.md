## Severity Calibration

- **P1**: The 40% auth theme budget reserves weight for auth beads even when no auth beads are in the dispatch queue — lower-priority themes cannot capture this idle capacity because the weight reservation is static, so the factory sits underutilized in other themes while auth capacity goes unused
  - When: When theme budget weights are applied as static multipliers at dispatch time rather than as dynamic allocations that release to the open pool when no eligible beads exist for that theme
- **P1**: Two Tier 2 CONSTRAIN signals fire simultaneously on different domains — the bypass condition is met — but the Tier 3 factory-paused.json mechanism writes a file that requires Clavain to check it on its next dispatch cycle, introducing a gap between the bypass trigger and the halt taking effect during which agents may claim additional beads under the assumption that factory operation is normal
  - When: When the Tier 3 halt mechanism is file-based and depends on dispatch-cycle polling rather than an active interrupt that takes effect before the next dispatch decision
- **P2**: A principal sets intent.yaml once at the start of a multi-day sprint and never updates it — the factory executes 80% of auth beads in day 1, leaving 20% unfinished, but Ockham continues applying 1.4x weight to the nearly-empty auth queue for days 2-3, artificially elevating a few remaining auth beads over higher-density themes
  - When: When ockham_weight computation uses a fixed budget allocation rather than a budget-remaining-vs-capacity-available ratio that adjusts as the theme's bead population is consumed
